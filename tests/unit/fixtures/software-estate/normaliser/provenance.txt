Normaliser fixture: s2_cases.tsv
================================

Loaded by tests/unit/server/test_product_normalize.cpp (the "S-2" test cases) through
YUZU_TEST_FIXTURE_DIR. Columns: kind (title or vendor), input, wanted normalised title or vendor,
wanted edition, wanted version, defect (1 to 7; 0 = a control row that must not change).

Where the inputs came from
--------------------------
The S-2 proxy spike (2026-11-03) ran the SAM.4 normaliser over the installed-software lists of
three developer machines: a macOS laptop, a Windows 11 host and Ubuntu 26.04 under WSL. Only the
product name and publisher strings were kept. The rows marked CAPTURED below are strings
present verbatim in that list; the rest are SYNTHETIC: written for this fixture to probe a rule
boundary or to pin a form that must not move, and not claimed to be captured from any device.

Issues (one per reproduced defect, plus two for behaviour this fixture pins and does not change)
  #8443  defect 1  a lone edition word normalises to an empty title
  #8444  defect 2  a leading edition word is taken as a false edition
  #8445  defect 3  "edition)" survives after an architecture word
  #8446  defect 4  a bare "-" separator is left in the title
  #8447  defect 5  vendor suffix words stripped anywhere, and a vendor reduced to nothing
  #8448  defect 6  versions inside names (name@version fixed; glued versions are a design limit)
  #8449  defect 7  "Plus" and the locale suffix stay in the title (not fixed)
  #8450  control   single-year Visual C++ titles share one title across generations (not changed)
  #8451  control   compound legal forms are not reduced (not changed)

Reproduction: against the normaliser at the base commit 0c68ac5043, 18 of the 34 rows fail: every
row of defects 1 to 5, the name@version rows of defect 6, the defect 7 row (by its dangling dash),
the three Visual C++ control rows (by their dangling dash) and the Example Software GmbH & Co. KG
control (it changes once, by design). All 34 pass after the fix. The glued-version rows of defect 6
(python3.14, postgresql-18, gcc-15) and the other controls pass on both: they pin behaviour that
must not move. The old output of each failing row is in the PR body.
