- **Engine credential `rotate` and `confirm`, and `DELETE /api/v1/engine-principals/{id}`, now tell clients when a `503` is safe to retry.** A `503` caused by a store that is not open or a store failure before anything changed carries `Retry-After: 2` and `retry_after_ms: 2000`; terminal answers (`400`, `403`, `404`, `409`) and a `503` after the change already committed (such as a dropped audit row) carry no hint.
