- **Dashboard actions that write an audit row first now decide their authority again after it, and the Echo purge and re-enable actions are bounded.** Creating a management group from results (`POST /api/dashboard/group-from-results`) decides the Search term's `Response:Read` admission and the Forensics tier again after its `attempted` row: a global `Forensics:Read` withdrawn while that row was written leaves in the group only the agents the caller may still read (the set is read again under the narrower tier), and a withdrawn Search admission or an undecidable tier creates nothing and records `response_read_revoked`, `forensics_tier_unknown`, `reread_unreadable` or `reread_truncated`. A tier that did not change, or widened, is not read again. On the Echo **Re-enable** and **Purge data** actions, a failed RBAC or management-group read while the per-device permission is decided is now answered `503` with an error toast and recorded `store_unavailable store=<mgmt_group_store|rbac_store>`, not collapsed into the `404` an out-of-scope device gets and recorded as a `scope_violation`; the scoped gate reports its own refusal class for this, its response and `auth.scoped_permission_required` row are unchanged, and an engine principal's degraded read is still recorded as a plain refusal. Each of the two actions is dispatched at most once per device and source per 30 seconds: a repeat answers `429` with `Retry-After`, writes no `attempted` row and is recorded `rate_limited`; the window is per server process. A session that ended before the scoped gate was asked is now recorded `denied` `session_revoked`, not a `scope_violation`.
