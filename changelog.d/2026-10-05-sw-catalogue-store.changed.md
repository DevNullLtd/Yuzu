- **Software catalogue rollup is now exact per filter and computes the page KPIs.** The `/software` catalogue rollup is precomputed per title for every kind/ecosystem/source filter combination, so Installs is an exact distinct-device count under any filter (a device carrying one title through two sources counts once). The hourly refresh now also computes the Software page KPI numbers (titles, publishers, installs by OS family, stay-current share over every title with a known version, version sprawl, rpm unsigned) and the fleet-newest version per title from one consistent snapshot; it is cancellable at shutdown and bounded by a 10-minute budget. The version drill honours the active filters and host. Search terms are matched literally (`%` and `_` no longer act as wildcards), clamped to 128 bytes and bounded by a 5 s query timeout. After upgrade the catalogue shows "building" until the first hourly refresh completes.
