- **A concurrent MFA disable during enrolment is no longer reported as a store outage (#3808).** If an operator's MFA was disabled (or disabled and re-enrolled) while they were confirming their first code, the confirm used to answer `503`, count a secret-unavailable degrade and write a critical "store unavailable" audit. It now reports that the enrolment was cancelled or replaced (`409` at `POST /login/mfa/enroll`, a "start enrollment again" message in Settings) and audits `mfa.enroll.cancelled`. Nothing is enrolled either way.
