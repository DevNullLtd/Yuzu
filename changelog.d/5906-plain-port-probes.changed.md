- **The plain-HTTP port answers the four health probes when HTTPS is on.** `GET /livez`, `/readyz`, `/health` and `/api/health` on the redirect port (8080 by default) are now served directly, in cleartext, to GET and HEAD requests, instead of answering `301`; every other request on that port still redirects. A monitor that expected the `301`, or followed it to TLS, now sees `200` (or `503`) and must repoint to `https://…:8443/readyz` or `yuzu-server --healthcheck`; a Kubernetes `httpGet` probe on this port needs no change (the kubelet followed the same-host redirect to TLS and now reads the direct answer). The HTTP port never resolves a session (`/health` always returns the unauthenticated shape) and answers `503` while the HTTPS listener is not bound. Responses on that port (the `301`s, `400`s and probe answers) now count in `yuzu_http_requests_total` with their own method and status labels, so `rate()` totals and the 5xx error ratio include them. They carry the same security headers as the HTTPS port minus HSTS, the port's worker pool is fixed-size, and shutdown waits at most 15 s for its thread. `--no-https-redirect` removes the listener.
