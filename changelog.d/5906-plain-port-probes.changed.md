- **The plain-HTTP port answers the four health probes when HTTPS is on.** `GET /livez`, `/readyz`, `/health` and `/api/health` on the redirect port (8080 by default) are now served directly, in cleartext, to GET and HEAD requests, instead of answering `301`; every other request on that port still redirects. A monitor that expected the `301`, or followed it to TLS, now sees `200` (or `503`) and must repoint to `https://…:8443/readyz` or `yuzu-server --healthcheck`; a Kubernetes `httpGet` probe on this port needs no change (the kubelet followed the same-host redirect to TLS and now reads the direct answer). The HTTP port never resolves a session (`/health` always returns the unauthenticated shape) and answers `503` while the HTTPS listener is not bound. `--no-https-redirect` removes the listener.
