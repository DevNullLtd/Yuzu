- **Workflows page: run, create and delete workflows.** On `/workflows`, **New workflow** takes the workflow's YAML, **Run now** opens a device checklist (with a device-id fallback when you may not list devices) and **Delete** retires a workflow after a confirmation. Controls you may not use are shown disabled with the permission they need. If the server takes longer than 8 seconds to answer a run, the page says the run may keep running, disables that workflow's Run now and watches its newest run for up to 10 minutes. Step rows show an instruction's name only when you also hold `InstructionDefinition:Read`.
