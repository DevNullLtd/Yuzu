- **Groups and enrolment page (`/groups`, Estate group).** Agents waiting for approval, auto-approve rules and enrolment tokens now have their own page in the redesigned layout, hosting the same three fragments the Settings page uses (same routes, element ids, form fields, audit rows and admin-only gate; Settings keeps all three panels). The lists are re-styled and gain honest states: tokens show Active, Expired, Exhausted or Revoked with UTC dates instead of raw epoch seconds; waiting agents show when they asked to join and can be forgotten from the queue; rules are described by what they match (a hostname is agent-reported and not verified; an address is IPv4 only) and a rule that matches every agent is flagged. "Approve all" and "Deny all" confirm with the total and the first ten hostnames and are disabled while the list shows only part of the queue, because the bulk routes act on every waiting agent. Enabling, disabling and removing a rule confirm in terms of the match mode (in "all must match" mode, enabling narrows and removing widens). A store that does not answer is shown as an unavailable state, never as an empty list. Failed actions now surface as toasts on the new page. Enrolment-token creation, batch creation, and the token and pending fragments are sent with `Cache-Control: no-store, private` and `Vary: Cookie`. The nav gains a "Groups and enrolment" entry first in Estate. Management groups stay in Settings.
