- **Real software titles stop minting empty, wrong-edition or split keys.** The title normaliser reads an edition word as an edition only after a title word (`Home` is a title, `Pro Display Calibrator` has no `pro` edition), drops `edition` after an architecture or edition word (`7-Zip 26.04 (x64 edition)` is `7-zip`), drops a bare `-` separator, and keeps the name of a Homebrew `name@version` (`openssl@3` is `openssl`, version `3`). The vendor normaliser strips only a trailing run of legal-suffix words and never the last word (`macOS Software Signing`, `Software AG` and `Software` keep their names; `L.L.C.` is a suffix). The seed alias pack is `2026.11.2`, gains rows for `python3.14` and `postgresql-18`, and re-keys two seed rows (`Microsoft .NET Runtime - 8.0.1 (x64)` and the Homebrew `openssl@3`); a server with a stored pack keeps its old keys until the pack is replaced.
