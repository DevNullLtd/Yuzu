- **A device's Hardware record no longer reads Offline when it is connected to another server.** The per-device record (the dashboard, `GET /api/v1/hardware/{id}` and MCP `get_hardware_ci`) took Online from the serving replica's local session map and showed every other device as Offline. Its offline fallback now asks the database whether the device is connected to any server, the same way the list does. (#7551)
