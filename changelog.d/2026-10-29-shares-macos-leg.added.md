- **The shares plugin now reads what a macOS host shares.** list on macOS reports the SMB share points the system records (through the bounded `sharing -l -f json` runner: name, path, read-only or read-write, guest access, with a share point whose SMB sharing is switched off flagged unavailable and one that requires SMB encryption flagged sealed) and the NFS exports in `/etc/exports`, parsed in Apple's mountd grammar on a best-effort basis (checks that need the host, such as resolving a host or network name or looking up an account, are not modelled; hosts counted, never listed; an export with no host and no network, or an IPv6 network with no or a zero mask, is flagged anonymous). Both sources are always reported, and a sharing tool that cannot start, times out, exits nonzero (other than printing its no-records text) or prints anything unrecognisable is reported as constrained with a named token, never as an empty list; ACLs, permissions and remarks are never read. This replaces the previous single unsupported/macos:planned row.
