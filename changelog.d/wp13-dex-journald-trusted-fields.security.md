- **Linux DEX journald signals are attributed by journald-stamped sender fields.** `process.crashed`, `service.crashed`, `service.hung` and `os.time_unsynced` were classified from client-settable journal fields, so any local user could forge them (#3872, #3871). They require `_PID=1` and `_UID=0` (unit failures), `_UID=0` plus the `systemd-coredump` identity (coredumps) and `_COMM=chronyd` plus the chrony `_SYSTEMD_UNIT` (clock), and the same terms are in the `journalctl` match. The coredump identity is from systemd documentation, not a live capture.
