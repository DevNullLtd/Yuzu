- **Access reviews on the Audit and reviews page.** The review campaign list, each campaign's frozen grants with a summary card, and the current grant population (on request, with a CSV download) now sit on `/access/audit`; `/access/audit?campaign=<id>` opens a campaign directly. Reviewers with `AccessReview:Attest` can open a review, attest or flag a grant and close a campaign from the page, through the existing `/api/v1/access-reviews` routes. Opening a review, closing a campaign and flagging a grant each ask for confirmation in a dialog; Attest records the decision as soon as it is clicked, and a decided grant offers no further controls on the page. Flag records evidence only and never revokes. Undecided grants are listed first, an unreadable store is shown as such, and viewing writes the same audit rows as the REST reads (`access_review.list`, `access_review.get`, and `access_review.exported` with `format=view`). Settings keeps its Access Reviews panel until the follow-up that retires it.
