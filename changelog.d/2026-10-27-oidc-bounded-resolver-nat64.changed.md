- **OIDC egress resolves names through a bounded resolver and shares one NAT64 discovery cache.** The `ipv4only.arpa` probe and (Linux and macOS) the host-name check of every OIDC fetch now run in the server's bounded resolver, which closes a use-after-free in httplib's timed `getaddrinfo` on glibc. NXDOMAIN or NODATA for `ipv4only.arpa` is now an answer ("no DNS64 here"): cached for the 1-minute negative interval, no warning. A busy resolver is inconclusive and never cached or logged; a real failure is warned about at most once a minute. The learned-prefix set is one process-wide never-shrinking cache that later features also read; an overflow (more than 64 advertised prefixes) refuses IPv6 OIDC fetches until restart or a fitting answer. DNS timeouts are exact milliseconds. New metrics `yuzu_server_nat64_discovery_*` and `yuzu_server_oidc_resolver_in_flight`. See [Upgrading](docs/user-manual/upgrading.md).
