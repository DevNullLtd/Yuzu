- **The DEX score policy has a stored home and is named on every Apps payload.** `GET /api/v1/dex/apps` and `list_dex_apps` now carry a `policy` block (source `default`|`custom`, revision, the four tier edges and names, and a `fault` reason when a stored document is invalid and the shipped defaults are in force) and a per-application `profile` marker; the policy lives under the runtime-config key `dex_score_policy`, and each server instance refreshes it at most every 5 seconds while it serves requests, so a change typically reaches every instance within 5 seconds. When the store cannot be read, the last good policy is used for up to 60 seconds and after that the Apps read answers a `503` ending `(score_policy_unavailable)` instead of scoring under a guess. `GET /api/config` lists `dex_score_policy` under a new `read_only_keys` field, and the generic `PUT /api/config/dex_score_policy` is refused with a `400` naming the policy route; the key is written through `PUT` and `DELETE /api/v1/dex/score-policy`, and until a policy is written every response reports `source: "default"`.
