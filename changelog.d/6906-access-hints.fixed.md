- **The Users and roles page now draws Assign role and the Enforce roles switch from the server's own administrator check (#6034).** The check is the durable-Administrator rule followed by the enforcement-regime check that the assignment and enforcement routes apply, not your account role. With enforcement on, an account whose role is `user` but which holds the `Administrator` grant now sees the controls, and an account administrator with no grant no longer sees a switch the server would refuse. When the check could not be answered, Assign role and the switch are drawn disabled with a reason and the per-row Remove controls are left out. Every click is still checked again by the server.
