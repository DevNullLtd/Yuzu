- **Clearing a second factor is one transaction, so its audit outcomes change (#6079, #8010).** The Settings **Disable two-factor** button and `yuzu-server --mfa-reset` now clear the factor, delete the account's recovery codes, end every durable session of the account, bump the session generation and write the outcome audit row in one database transaction (`MfaDisableOwner`): all of it commits or none of it does. The `attempted` intent row is still written first, outside the transaction, and still fails closed (the CLI exits `1` with `error: audit store did not accept the intent row; nothing was changed`; Settings answers `503` with `Sec-Audit-Failed: true`). The `partial` outcome and the `sessions_ended=failed` detail no longer exist for `mfa.disabled` and `mfa.reset.breakglass`: a clear whose sessions cannot be ended rolls back and leaves the factor enrolled. The Settings outcome rows are now `ok` with `sessions_ended=<n>` (or `ok` with `no verified factor to clear` when an unfinished enrolment is cancelled, which ends no session), `denied` with `reason=factor_changed expected_gen=<n>`, and `error` with `disable failed` (`503`, nothing changed); an outcome row the transaction cannot store answers `503` with `Sec-Audit-Failed: true` and changes nothing. The `--mfa-reset` outcome is `success` (text unchanged) or `error`, and every `error` says nothing was changed: `MFA clear refused; session store did not open, nothing changed`, `MFA clear refused; no active account, nothing changed`, `MFA clear failed; audit store refused the outcome row, nothing changed` or `MFA clear failed; rolled back, re-run after checking status`, each followed by `(os_identity=<account>)`, with matching `error: ...; nothing was changed` lines on stderr (the last adds `unless the connection was lost at commit`). The old `error` detail `MFA clear failed; state unknown, re-run after checking status` and the stderr line `error: failed to clear MFA; the state is unknown, ...` are gone. An `attempted` row with no outcome row now means the clear did not commit and the factor is intact; the one indeterminate case is a connection lost exactly at COMMIT, where the clear, the session delete and the outcome row either all landed or none did. SIEM rules keyed on `result IN ('partial','error')` should key on `result = 'error'`. The Settings enrolment confirm (`POST /api/settings/mfa/verify`) now refuses to enrol unless the caller's session is a valid stored session: `401` with audit `mfa.enroll.failed` detail `session_not_durable` (sign in again), or `503` with `Retry-After: 5` and detail `session_store_unavailable` when the session store cannot be read; the provisional secret is untouched either way. API tokens are still not ended by a clear.
