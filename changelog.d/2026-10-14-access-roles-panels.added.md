- **Roles and role permissions on the Users and roles page.** A **Roles** panel lists the role catalogue and **View permissions** shows what one role allows, grouped by area. Both use the same permission (`UserManagement:Read`) and the same checked read as the REST role endpoints, so an unreadable role store is a red message rather than an empty list, and an unknown role is told apart from a role with no permissions. Unlike the Users panels, a refused Roles load is recorded in the audit log as a permission-required denial.
