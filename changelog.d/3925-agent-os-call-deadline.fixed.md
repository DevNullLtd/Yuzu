- **Windows share, account, session, snapshot, domain-join and adapter reads that could hang the agent now have a deadline.** The reads behind `shares.list`, `users` (`logged_on`, `sessions`, `local_users`, `local_admins`, `group_members`), `filesystem_posture.snapshots`, `device_identity.domain` and `network_config` (`adapters`, `ip_addresses`, `dns_servers`), and Echo's user and inbound mapped-drive reads, now run in the agent core with a time limit: 5 seconds per read, the group and administrator reads included (per page for `shares.list`), 10 seconds for the local account list and 15 seconds for volume snapshots. A hung Windows service no longer holds an agent worker past that limit: the action answers at the deadline with a constrained result, the cause (`deadline_exceeded`, `abandoned_call_pending` or `call_in_progress`) is in the result rows where the action writes it there and otherwise in the agent log, later calls answer at once until the stuck call returns, and Echo skips that tick instead of recording a change. A `device_identity.domain` read with no answer also skips that `device_ci` daily-sync cycle. Account and member lists are cut at 16,384 entries and the session list at 4,096 sessions; a cut list is reported constrained, and Echo records no `user` events from one. On Linux and macOS `users.local_users` stops starting per-account tools after 30 seconds and reports the remaining accounts with unknown fields (on macOS their `description` reads `-`) (#3925).
