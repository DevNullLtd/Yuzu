- **Decommissioning a device now erases its Guardian compliance-census rows (#5675).** `DELETE /api/v1/sle/agents/{id}` fans out across nine stores, and the response's `stores` map gains a `guaranteed_state` key. For that store, `deleted` means the device's compliance-census rows were erased and its pending census evidence retired; the Guardian event log and DEX observations are not erased by the decommission and expire by retention. A decommissioned agent therefore stops counting in `errored_rules` and `uncertain_rules`, and the `yuzu_server_guardian_census_pending_events` gauge drops; re-registering the same `agent_id` after a decommission inherits no stale errored state, while a bare re-register without one keeps the old row by design. A still-running agent re-creates its census row on its next census-bearing event, and a journal replay cannot rebuild the census from the retired evidence, so stop the agent first. During a rolling upgrade a replica that has not been upgraded returns `200` without a `guaranteed_state` key and did not erase the census rows; re-issue the DELETE once every replica is upgraded. An agent that is merely offline still counts with its last reported state, as before.
