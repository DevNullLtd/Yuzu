- **Breaking — access-review decisions are now final, and a reviewer cannot decide their own grant (#4752).** `record_attestation` (MCP tool and `POST /api/v1/access-reviews/{id}/attestations`) let any holder of `AccessReview:Attest`, including the non-admin `Reviewer` role, sign off a grant held under their own username, and let a grant's subject replace another reviewer's `flagged_revoke` with `attested` while the campaign was open, so the closed campaign showed only the self-signed result. Now the first decision on a grant sticks (a second call returns `409`, MCP invalid-params, and the message names the decision already recorded), and a reviewer who is the subject of a `user` grant, or the owner of an `engine` principal as recorded when the campaign opened, gets `403` (MCP permission-denied). Both rules are enforced in the same database write as the decision, so every caller inherits them, and a decision can no longer land in a campaign that is being closed. An identifier with a NUL byte or over 512 bytes is refused with `400` instead of reaching the audit trail. A refusal is audited `result=denied` under `access_review.attested` / `access_review.flagged` (a SIEM rule keyed on the verb alone now sees those rows too). Scripts and agents that re-send, correct or flip a decision, or attest a grant of the account they run as, now get `409` or `403`; a retry after a lost response gets `409`. Decisions already recorded, including earlier self-attestations, are not invalidated; upgrade every replica before opening or deciding campaigns, and see `docs/user-manual/upgrading.md` for the query that finds earlier self-attestations. This does not cover group grants, one person holding more than one account, or ownership changed after a campaign opened, and it needs at least two people able to attest (a sole Administrator cannot decide their own grants). A mistaken decision is fixed by opening a new campaign. Also closes the silent last-write-wins between two concurrent reviewers (#2326 item 2), fixes the Settings Access reviews panel printing "[object Object]" for a refused action, and reloads a campaign after a `409`.
