- **Access-review attestations now enforce separation of duties and are final (#4752).** `record_attestation` (MCP tool and `POST /api/v1/access-reviews/{id}/attestations`) let any holder of `AccessReview:Attest`, including the non-admin `Reviewer` role, sign off their own grant, and let the subject of a grant replace another reviewer's `flagged_revoke` with `attested` while the campaign was open, so the closed campaign showed only the self-signed result. The reviewer may now not be the subject of a `user` grant or the owner of an `engine` principal (as recorded when the campaign opened), and the first decision on a grant sticks. Both rules are enforced in the same statement as the write, so every caller inherits them. A refused call returns `403` (self-attestation) or `409` (already decided) on REST and permission-denied or invalid-params on MCP, and is audited `result=denied` under `access_review.attested` / `access_review.flagged`. A mistaken decision is fixed by opening a new campaign. Group grants have no owner and are not covered by the owner check. Also closes the silent last-write-wins between two concurrent reviewers (#2326 item 2).
