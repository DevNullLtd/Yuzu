- **Gateway: `mgmt_crl_max_validity_s` bounds how long a CRL may claim to be valid (#7070).** A validly signed CRL whose `nextUpdate` is more than that many seconds after its `thisUpdate` is refused `validity_too_long` and the CRL already held stays in force, so a far-future `nextUpdate` can no longer keep `YuzuGatewayMgmtCrlStale` quiet. The width is read from the CRL's own two dates and is never compared with the gateway's clock. Unset in code (no bound); `deploy/docker/reference-gateway-sys.config` sets `691200` (8 days, above the 7 days a server publishing 7-day CRLs needs) and `sys.config.prod` carries it as a commented example. An out-of-range value or a float (valid: an integer from 3600 to 31536000) refuses boot; set with no `mgmt_crl_source`, the gateway logs one NOTICE line saying the key has no effect. A CRL source whose CRLs are valid for more than 8 days must raise the key or leave it unset, or its CRLs are refused `validity_too_long`. The gateway now also logs a WARNING (at most hourly) once a fetched CRL passes 12 MiB, 75 percent of the 16 MiB bound, and `gateway.md`/ADR 2004 record the measured cost of parsing a CRL at the bound: about 1.1 GiB of transient memory (1,111 to 1,153 MiB measured; plan for 1.2 GiB) and 2 to 2.5 seconds in the watcher.
