- **Engine credential `rotate` and `confirm`, and `DELETE /api/v1/engine-principals/{id}`, now tell clients when a `503` is safe to retry.** A `503` from a store that is not open, or from a store failure that leaves the work unfinished and safe to repeat (for a revoke, that includes the credentials being revoked while the identity revoke failed), carries `Retry-After: 2` and `retry_after_ms: 2000`. Terminal answers (`400`, `403`, `404`, `409`) and a `503` after the change committed (such as a dropped audit row) carry no hint. A `confirm` on two active credentials that are not a linked rotation pair now answers `409` instead of a retryable `503`, because retrying cannot repair it.
- The served OpenAPI document now names every outcome the quarantine, engine-principal revoke and credential rotate and confirm, session revoke, upload-grant mint, `POST /elevate` and v2 plugin-policy routes can return: both quarantine 403 refusals and the audit-unavailable 503, the six revoke 503 causes with which ones carry a retry hint, the sessions 500 and 503, the two missing upload-grant 400 causes, every `/elevate` refusal (a SAML session is always refused), and the plugin-policy permission (granted to Administrator by default, not Administrator-only).
