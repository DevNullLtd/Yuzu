- **Two sample alert rules: Echo corruption rise and plugin init failure** (#6696, #5065). `YuzuEchoDbCorruptionDetected` fires when the count of agents with a quarantined Echo database rises over an hour (a net rise in agents reporting a quarantine history, not an event count), and `YuzuPluginInitFailed` fires when agents report a plugin that failed init for a little over 10 minutes (`for: 15m` over a 5-minute carry), with the Echo remedies in its description. Both ship with promtool cases and negative controls. No alert covers the on-demand `echo.status` registry keys yet; that needs a fleet gauge and is recorded as a deferred follow-up.
