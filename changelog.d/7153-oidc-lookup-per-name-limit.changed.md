- **Behaviour change: OIDC name lookups are limited to 8 outstanding per name.** At most 8 lookups for the same host name are outstanding at once, and at most 64 names have one. A lookup beyond either fails at once with "the host name lookup was not started", takes no pool slot, and is counted as `busy` in `yuzu_server_oidc_resolver_lookups_total`. A lookup counts against its name until the resolver returns, even after its caller stopped waiting at the time bound. A lookup refused by the limit is not remembered, a discovery refused that way arms no 30 s window, and the NAT64 probe is limited in the same way. There are no new flags. Windows builds are unchanged. See `docs/user-manual/upgrading.md`.
