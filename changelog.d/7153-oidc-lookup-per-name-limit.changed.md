- **Behaviour change: OIDC name lookups are limited to 8 outstanding per name.** At most 8 lookups for the same host name are outstanding at once, and at most 64 names have one. A lookup beyond either fails at once with "the host name lookup was not started", takes no pool slot, and is counted as `busy` in `yuzu_server_oidc_resolver_lookups_total`. A lookup counts against its name until the resolver returns, even after its caller stopped waiting at the time bound. A lookup refused by the limit is not remembered, a discovery refused that way arms no 30 s window, and the NAT64 probe is exempt from the limit (only the pool's cap can refuse it, and that cap is its only bound). A token exchange refused by the limit or by the table of 64 names ends that sign-in (the user starts a new one). A name stuck at the limit reads about 8 of the 16 slots (a single name holds at most 8, except the NAT64 probe name, which only the pool's cap bounds), so the configured issuer name now tolerates 8 stuck lookups where it tolerated 16; if the resolver is answering again and the name stays refused, restart the server (one replica at a time under high availability). There are no new flags. Windows builds are unchanged. See `docs/user-manual/upgrading.md`.
