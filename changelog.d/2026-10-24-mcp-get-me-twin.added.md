- **New MCP tool `get_me`, the twin of `GET /api/v1/me`.** An agentic worker can read its own identity, how it authenticated and its directly assigned RBAC roles without a permission probe. `GET /api/v1/me` and `GET /api/me` gain `auth_source` (always present), `mcp_tier`, `scope_service`, `rbac_roles` and `rbac_roles_resolved`, and `principal_kind` now reads `human` or `engine`. `get_me` refuses service-scoped tokens; the REST route still lets them describe themselves. Refs #2146.
