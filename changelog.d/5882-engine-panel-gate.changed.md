- **Breaking: the Settings Engine Principals panel now needs `EnginePrincipal:Read`, as `GET /api/v1/engine-principals` does (#5882).** With RBAC enabled, an account administrator who does not hold the permission (for example one whose only role is a custom role) is refused and sees "You do not have permission to view this panel" where the list was; grant `EnginePrincipal:Read` (the built-in `Administrator` and `Viewer` roles hold it) to restore it. Newly admitted: with RBAC enabled, a non-administrator who holds the permission, such as a `Viewer`, can read the panel at its URL although the `/settings` page stays administrator-only. An MCP-tier token is still refused by the panel. The panel now writes one `engine_principal.list` success row per view, as the route does, and the revoke Detail of a revoked principal shows the original recorded detail to callers who also hold `AuditLog:Read`. A refused browser session writes no denial row (the panel auto-loads); a refused token or engine credential does. See [upgrading](docs/user-manual/upgrading.md).
