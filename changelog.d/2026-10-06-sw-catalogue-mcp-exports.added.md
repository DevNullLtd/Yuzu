- **Software catalogue MCP twins and raw-route pagination and exports.** MCP tools `query_software_catalogue` and `get_software_versions` share the REST catalogue's model. `GET /api/v1/inventory/software` and `query_installed_software` gain a `version` filter, a `source` field per row and opaque `cursor`/`next_cursor` pagination; the REST route also gains `q`, `kind`, `ecosystem` and `source` filters and a `format=csv|json` export of up to 10000 rows (REST-only; a CSV export carries its continuation in an `X-Next-Cursor` header and ends with a `# result_truncated_by_cap` record when more rows may exist).
