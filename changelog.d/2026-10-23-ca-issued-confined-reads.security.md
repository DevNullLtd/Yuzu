- **Breaking — an operator whose Inventory access is granted only through management groups is now refused the issued-certificate list.** `GET /api/v1/ca/issued` without a filter, the MCP tool `list_issued_certs` without filter arguments, and the issued-certificate list in the Settings certificate panel returned every certificate's subject, SAN and issuing identity to any caller holding `Security:Read`, including one confined this way. The affected shape is a role with global `Security:Read` and no global `Inventory:Read`, plus a management-group-scoped `Inventory:Read`; the seeded Viewer and Administrator roles hold a global `Inventory:Read` and are not confined. They now apply the check the filtered form already used: that caller gets `403` (MCP: permission denied) with no permission named, and the panel withholds only the list (a note in its place; the CA details, the root and CRL downloads and the import form are still shown, and a revoke or import answers the panel with a confirmation banner instead of a blank body). An access check that reports itself unavailable is `503` with `retry_after_ms: 5000`. **What did not change:** RBAC-off installs (the default) are unchanged; a `Security:Read` holder with no `Inventory:Read` grant at all is not confined and is served the list (whether it should be is open, #6383; removing a scoped grant does not confine a caller, and granting a global `Inventory:Read` removes the confinement); and a read failure partway through the access check **fails open**, serving the list, on the unfiltered list, the filtered list and the `/crypto` drill; that is tracked by #6382. Both forms are now audited `crypto.ca.certificates.view` (target `inventory`): a `success` row per page ending `standing=available|refused`, a `denied` row for a refusal and a `failure` row when the access check or the store is unavailable. A `standing=refused` row cannot tell a caller with no Inventory grant from a read failure inside the check, and a row may be missing during the same outage that caused the failure. The Settings panel itself is still not audited.
