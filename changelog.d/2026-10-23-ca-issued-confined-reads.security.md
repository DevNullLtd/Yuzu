- **Breaking — an operator whose Inventory access is granted only through management groups is now refused the issued-certificate list.** `GET /api/v1/ca/issued` without a filter, the MCP tool `list_issued_certs` without filter arguments, and the issued-certificate list in the Settings certificate panel returned every certificate's subject, SAN and issuing identity to any caller holding `Security:Read`, including one confined this way. The affected shape is a role with global `Security:Read` and no global `Inventory:Read`, plus a management-group-scoped `Inventory:Read`; the seeded Viewer and Administrator roles hold a global `Inventory:Read` and are not confined. They now apply the check the filtered form already used: that caller gets `403` (MCP: permission denied) with no permission named, and the panel withholds only the list (a note in its place; the CA details, the root and CRL downloads and the import form are still shown, and a revoke or import answers the panel with a confirmation banner instead of a blank body). An access check that reports itself unavailable is `503` with `retry_after_ms: 5000`. A `Security:Read` holder with **no** `Inventory:Read` grant at all is refused too (a custom role that holds `Security:Read` alone is the affected shape; holding no grant is not an affirmative admission to a fleet-wide list, and removing a caller's last scoped grant never widens what it can see). A read failure partway through the access check is a retryable `503 access check unavailable` on the unfiltered list, the filtered list and the `/crypto` drill (a note or banner in the panel and the drill), never the list; the shared list-read check reports a failed read separately (`read_failed`), so the fleet-read gate answers `503` with `retry_after_ms: 5000` instead of a `403` for every fleet-gated read. **What did not change:** RBAC-off installs (the default) are unchanged. Both forms are now audited `crypto.ca.certificates.view` (target `inventory`): a `success` row per page ending `standing=available`, a `denied` row for a refusal (`withheld=confined` or `withheld=refused`) and a `failure` row when the access check or the store is unavailable. A row may be missing during the same outage that caused a failure. The Settings panel itself is still not audited.
