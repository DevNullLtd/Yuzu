- **Echo loads offline and retries when its v10 migration fails.** A failed roll-up step (a damaged mark, a bucket outside years 0001-9999, a locked file, a full volume, an attempt that ran past its deadline) no longer fails the Echo plugin or needs an agent restart: `echo.status` reports `storage_state|offline|migration_failed`, the file is left exactly as it was, and the agent retries the open at 1, 5 and 15 minutes and then hourly. The free-space refusal retries the same way. Each attempt is bounded by a deadline of 60 seconds plus 2 seconds per MiB of the file (doubled for every attempt that ran out of time, capped at one hour), and the migration re-reads the schema version and the retired tables inside its transaction, so a second opener that finishes first is harmless. Fleet visibility: the heartbeat tag `yuzu.plugin.echo.storage_offline`, the gauge `yuzu_fleet_echo_storage_offline{reason}` and the alert `YuzuEchoStorageOffline` (warning, held 30 minutes).
