- **`GET /api/v1/me` and `GET /api/me` no longer invent a role.** An engine principal no longer gets a made-up `Viewer` in `rbac_role`; its grants appear in `rbac_roles` and `rbac_role` is absent when it has none. When the role store cannot be read, the call answers `200` with `rbac_roles_resolved: false` and no role keys instead of a made-up role. `rbac_role` is deprecated in favour of `rbac_roles`.
