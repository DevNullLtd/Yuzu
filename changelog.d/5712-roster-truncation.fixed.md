- **The Hardware list, its KPIs, `GET /api/v1/hardware` and MCP `list_hardware_ci` now say when the roster is truncated at 100,000 endpoints.** The roster read keeps the newest 100,000 endpoints seen in its window (30 days for Hardware) and used to present them as the whole fleet. A fleet over that size now sees a **"Partial list"** notice above the table, the four KPI count tiles marked "at least", and `roster_truncated: true` with `roster_cap: 100000` in the REST and MCP bodies; `total_matching`, `pagination.total`, the REST `devices_omitted` and `kpis` are lower bounds then, and a search that finds nothing does not prove a device is absent. The notice is worked out afresh on every read and clears by itself when the fleet is back under the cap, and `roster_truncated=1` is recorded on the audit detail. Fleet topology (REST and MCP) carries `offline_roster_truncated` only when a request is allowed past `machines_max` (it is a `413` otherwise, #7550). The Software and Quarantine hostname column stays display-only and silent (it shows the `agent_id`), and the Hardware CI columns have a separate cap (#7549). The Dashboard's scoped device estate takes its "at least" label from the same typed flag instead of guessing from a row count, so a fleet of exactly 100,000 is no longer labelled partial. **A device's offline identity** (the Hardware record, the decommission confirmation hostname, the containment lens) is now an exact point read by `agent_id` in the same window instead of a scan of the capped roster, so an enrolled offline device older than the newest 100,000 is no longer reported "not found" (REST `404`); its failures count under `yuzu_server_agent_presence_store_failed_total{op="find_within"}`.
