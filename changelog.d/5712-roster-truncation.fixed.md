- **The Hardware roster no longer silently truncates at 100,000 endpoints.** The roster read (the Hardware list and its KPI strip, `GET /api/v1/hardware`, MCP `list_hardware_ci`, the Software and Quarantine hostname column, and the offline merge in the fleet topology on REST and MCP) kept the newest 100,000 endpoints seen in the 30-day window and presented them as the whole fleet. A fleet over that size now sees a **"Partial list"** notice above the table, the KPI counts marked "at least", and `roster_truncated: true` with `roster_cap: 100000` in the REST and MCP bodies (`offline_roster_truncated: true` on the topology). The list is still served: a partial is never a refusal, the notice is worked out afresh on every read and clears by itself when the fleet is back under the cap, and `roster_truncated=1` / `offline_roster_truncated=1` is recorded on the audit detail. `total_matching`, `pagination.total`, `devices_omitted` and `kpis` are lower bounds then, and a search that finds nothing does not prove a device is absent. The Dashboard's scoped device estate now takes its "at least" label from the same typed flag instead of guessing from a row count, so a fleet of exactly 100,000 is no longer labelled partial. A new unlabelled counter, `yuzu_server_agent_presence_roster_truncated_total`, counts truncated reads (no alert: it is a steady state on such a fleet). **A device's offline identity** (the Hardware record, the decommission confirmation hostname, the containment lens) is now a direct lookup by `agent_id` in the same window instead of a scan of the capped roster, so an enrolled offline device past the newest 100,000 no longer reads as "not found" (REST `404`); its failures count under `yuzu_server_agent_presence_store_failed_total{op="find_within"}`. Additive only: no existing field changed. Closes #5712.
