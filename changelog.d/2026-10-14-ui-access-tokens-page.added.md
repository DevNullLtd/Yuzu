- Tokens and principals page (`/access/tokens`, new Access group in the navigation): read-only list of API tokens (Active / All, rotation and expiry state, engine credentials marked) and of engine principals with their active credentials and roles, plus the no-administrator role check. Principal panels and the check are requested only for an administrator on a cookie session; every other caller, including an administrator's API or MCP token, sees their own tokens only. A read that does not answer is stated as such, never rendered as empty. Creating, rotating and revoking stay in Settings. New dashboard fragments `/fragments/access/tokens/list`, `/fragments/access/principals/list` and `/fragments/access/principals/detail`.
