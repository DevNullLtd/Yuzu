- **The Hardware list no longer shows a device as having no hardware record because of where its hostname sorts.** The CI columns came from a read of the whole `device_ci` table ordered by hostname and cut at 100,000 rows, so on a fleet past that size every device after the cut showed blank serial, model, CPU and RAM, with no warning, and the CI-coverage figure undercounted. The list now reads CI only for the devices on its roster, so the read is bounded by the roster and cannot drop a listed device. If that read fails or runs out of its 20-second budget the list shows the existing "CI columns unavailable" notice (`ci_degraded`), never a partial result. The Partial-list notice no longer says CI columns can be blank "even when no CI warning shows". (#7549)
