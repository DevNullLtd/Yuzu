- **`GET /api/v1/hardware/{id}` and the MCP `get_hardware_ci` tool now serve the expanded hardware record.** `ci` gains the chassis (type, serial, asset tag, source), baseboard (vendor, product, serial, source) and hashed machine-id (hash, algorithm, source) values, and a `sections` array with each reported section's `status`, `reason`, `source`, `complete` flag and rows (cpu, dimm, disk, swap, volume, partition, topology; chassis, baseboard and machine_id are status only). Each section's row count and a server-computed content digest of its rows are now recorded when the device syncs in full (two new nullable columns, added by an automatic additive database migration), and a section whose stored rows no longer match them, for example after a partial database restore or rows edited out of band, is served `constrained` and incomplete with reason `server:row_count_mismatch` or `server:content_digest_mismatch` rather than as a complete section (under `server:content_digest_mismatch` the rows are served but are not the values the agent reported; the digest is an integrity check against accident and non-malicious drift, not tamper resistance); a section stored before this change is unverified until that device's next full sync. A `constrained` section reads as incomplete rather than as a device with fewer rows, and an `unavailable` or `permission_denied` section shows its reason and no rows. A new `sections_state` (`found`, `absent`, `degraded`) tells "no sections stored" (an older agent) from a record whose `ci` could not be read. The record and its sections are read in one consistent snapshot, and on a failed read of the sections both surfaces answer a retryable failure with `retry_after_ms` 5000 and withhold the record (`503` on REST, a retryable error on MCP); clients of `GET /api/v1/hardware/{id}` must handle it. The MCP output schema declares the new keys. The Hardware list search (`q`, on the REST route, the MCP `list_hardware_ci` tool and the dashboard) also matches asset tag, chassis serial and board serial. The OpenAPI document describes the new keys and the new `HardwareCiSection` schema.
