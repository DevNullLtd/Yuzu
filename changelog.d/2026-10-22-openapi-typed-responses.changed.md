- **The OpenAPI document now types the success response of 257 of its 259 operations, and every operation has a unique `operationId`.** Before, 244 operations described their 2xx body as a prose string. Each now declares a `content` schema (284 component schemas in all, including `InstalledSoftwarePage`, `HardwareListPage`, `HardwareRecord` and the `Dex*`, `Security*` and `Execution*` families) derived from the handler and the serialiser it calls. Where the handler's real wrapper differed from the old prose (no `data`/`meta` on the CA, KEK, upload, offload-target and `discover` routes; `pagination.total` is the returned row count and `page_size` is always 50 on `list_json` routes), the schema follows the code. `GET /access-reviews/export`, `GET /responses/{id}/export` and `GET /inventory/software/catalogue` also declare their `text/csv` form. `PUT /management-groups/{id}`, `POST` and `DELETE /management-groups/{id}/roles`, `POST /management-groups/{id}/members` and `PUT /uploads/{upload_id}/chunk` now declare the request body the handler reads. The two operations still without a response schema, `DELETE /upload-grants/{grant_id}` and `DELETE /uploads/{upload_id}`, send no body. Error responses are still described in prose.
