- **Breaking - generated clients and byte-level pins are affected: the OpenAPI document now types the success response of 268 of its 270 operations, and every operation has a unique `operationId`.** Before, 229 operations had no schema on their 2xx response (they described the body as a prose string). 227 of them now declare a `content` schema (the other two are the `204` deletes named below), and the 29 operations added since the previous release are all typed, with 296 component schemas in all, including `InstalledSoftwarePage`, `HardwareListPage`, `HardwareRecord` and the `Dex*`, `Security*` and `Execution*` families, derived from the handler and the serialiser it calls. Where the handler's real wrapper differed from the old prose (no `data`/`meta` wrapper on the offload-target routes, the `discover` routes, and every upload route except `GET /upload-grants`, which does wrap; top-level keys plus `meta` on the CA revoke, import-chain and issue-code-signing routes and the KEK routes; on list routes, `pagination.total` is the number of rows returned, `page_size` is a fixed 50 except on `GET /audit`, where it echoes the `limit` parameter, and `start` is the request offset on `GET /audit` and `GET /guaranteed-state/events` and 0 elsewhere), the schema follows the code. `GET /inventory/software`, `GET /inventory/software/catalogue`, `GET /access-reviews/export` and `GET /responses/{id}/export` also declare their `text/csv` form. `PUT /management-groups/{id}`, `POST` and `DELETE /management-groups/{id}/roles`, `POST /management-groups/{id}/members` and `PUT /uploads/{upload_id}/chunk` now declare the request body the handler reads. The two operations still without a response schema, `DELETE /upload-grants/{grant_id}` and `DELETE /uploads/{upload_id}`, send no body. Error responses are still described in prose.
- **The default OpenAPI document is larger.** Because the typed responses and request bodies are now in the document, `GET /api/v1/openapi.json` without `?guides=true`, and so the MCP resource `yuzu://openapi`, grew from about 314 KB to about 670 KB (roughly double), measured on the build under review. The guides are not part of that growth, since the default form leaves them out. `?guides=true` accepts exactly `true` or `1`, in lower case, and returns the document as written, while the default form is parsed and written out again, so key order and whitespace differ between the two although the content is the same apart from the guides. Every operation now also carries an `operationId`, and the 14 operations the running server does not serve carry an `x-yuzu-unavailable` note.
