- **Guardian (agent, spark backend only): a rule whose arm hung and then returned late is no longer waived while its compensating teardown is outstanding.** Dormant until `prefer_spark` is enabled, which no released build does. Previously the policy generation could be acknowledged after three identical re-applies while the agent still owed a teardown of the late subscription. That stopped the server's re-push and left the rule unarmed with nothing to re-arm it. Now the generation is held, not acknowledged, until the teardown returns or the agent restarts. While it is held the server keeps re-pushing at most once per 25 s per agent (about once per 30 s heartbeat), and each push writes a `guaranteed_state.reconcile` audit row; the earlier bound of roughly 75 to 90 s (an estimate) no longer applies to this case. `yuzu.guardian_compensation_pending_age_seconds` and `yuzu.guardian_compensation_deadline_elapsed` show it, with the deadline 5 s after the compensation became owed. Whether a waived arm that later fails should recover automatically is an open decision (#5459) (#4472).
