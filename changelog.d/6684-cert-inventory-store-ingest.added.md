- **Fleet certificate inventory store and ingest (server side).** The server now persists the `certificate_inventory` daily-sync source in a new Postgres schema, `certificate_inventory_store`, on both the direct `ReportInventory` and the gateway `ProxyInventory` paths through one shared ingest seam. Per-store replace rules keep a partly read or unreadable store's rows instead of ageing them away; a hash-skip refreshes only what the last full report carried; a record over a cap is refused, never truncated, and invalid UTF-8 is replaced and marked. The store joins the agent-decommission cascade (now eight stores) and `/readyz`, and ingest is counted on `yuzu_server_cert_inventory_ingest_total{result,reason}`. There is no read surface and no agent collector in this change. Reports for one device are serialised by a Postgres advisory lock that ingest only tries (a busy device is answered `deferred`/`agent_busy`). Upgrade note: the decommission response gains a `certificate_inventory` key (eight stores), and erasure is not durable until the decommission tombstone ships. Each device also gets a server-assigned random sample key at first ingest (never exposed), used later to sample reporters of a certificate in an order an agent cannot choose.
