- **Guardian (agent, spark backend only): a large policy no longer has its surplus rules refused outright when many rules of one kind arm at once.** Until now a rule beyond the per-kind start limit (Service 3, File 4, Registry 3) was refused at arm dispatch and stayed unenforced until the next policy push. Such a rule now waits for a free slot and is started as earlier ones finish. It keeps its existing claim deadline (5 s by default), observed at the next heartbeat maintenance pass (every 30 s by default), so a large enough surplus of slow-starting rules can still expire (logged as `status=CongestionExpired`) and recover on the next successful re-apply. This affects the spark backend only, which is not enabled in any released build (`prefer_spark` is off), so shipped agents are unchanged. The compensating-disarm safety guarantee is unchanged (#5168).
