- **DEX app performance: per-version crash and hang join.** `GET /api/v1/dex/perf/app` and the MCP tool `get_dex_app_perf` now carry a `versions[]` array joining each version with the crash and hang observations of the last 30 days: fleet-wide counts and affected devices (breadth), the exposure (devices with retained resource-significant samples, labelled as such), the crash-and-hang rate among exposed devices with its exact 95 percent interval, and the rate ratio against the previous canonical version with an exact interval.
