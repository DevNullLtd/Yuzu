- **DEX app performance: per-version crash and hang join.** `GET /api/v1/dex/perf/app` and the MCP tool `get_dex_app_perf` now carry a `versions[]` array joining each version with the crash and hang observations of up to the last 30 days (clamped inside the observation retention): fleet-wide counts and affected devices (breadth), the exposure (devices with retained resource-significant samples, labelled as such), the crash-and-hang rate among exposed devices with its exact 95 percent interval, and the rate ratio against the previous canonical version with an exact interval. The crash and hang counts are each withheld when the devices behind them (or the devices outside the exposure cohort) number fewer than 10, and the exposed figures when the exposure cohort is under 10. The route now answers `503` when either underlying read degrades, so a caller that reads only `points[]` must handle it. Additive otherwise; existing fields are unchanged.
