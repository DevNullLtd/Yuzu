- **The DEX score policy can now be read, replaced and reset over MCP.** Three new tools, `get_dex_score_policy`, `set_dex_score_policy` and `reset_dex_score_policy`, mirror `GET`, `PUT` and `DELETE /api/v1/dex/score-policy` and share the one write function behind them, so validation, the required `If-Match` revision, `dry_run` and the audit rows are identical. `get_dex_score_policy` is readable at every MCP tier. `set_dex_score_policy` and `reset_dex_score_policy` need a supervised-tier token with `GuaranteedState:Read` as well as `Infrastructure:Write` (`Infrastructure:Delete` for reset), and a reset also needs an approval ticket; a failure after approval spends the ticket, and the error says to read the policy and request a fresh one. An audit row dropped after the change is reported as `audit_persisted: false`. Do not use `set_dex_score_policy` during a rolling upgrade or rollback: a document carrying `model` is stored as version 2, which an older replica refuses and scores under the shipped defaults until the roll ends.
