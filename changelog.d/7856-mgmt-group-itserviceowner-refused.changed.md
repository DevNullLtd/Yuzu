- **The management-group store refuses a group-scoped `ITServiceOwner` grant.** No route could create one before (the management-group role routes accept only `Operator` and `Viewer`), so no API behaviour changes. Existing rows are not rewritten: they still count toward group visibility and group-owner authority until removed, and removing one through the role `DELETE` route needs the global `ManagementGroup:Write` permission. The seeded `ITServiceOwner` role and its use as the authority ceiling for service-scoped API tokens are unchanged.
