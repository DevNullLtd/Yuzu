- **Software installs read: a confined caller's scope is applied in SQL.** `GET /api/v1/inventory/software` and `query_installed_software` push the caller's visible devices (up to 10000) into the query, so a confined caller receives complete pages of its own devices. `devices_omitted` now counts only the devices hidden by the post-filter used for a wider scope, so it is `0` under push-down and no longer signals that devices exist outside the caller's scope; a `denied` audit row is written only when it is above zero. A non-integer `limit` such as `12abc` is now rejected with `400` instead of being read as `12`.
