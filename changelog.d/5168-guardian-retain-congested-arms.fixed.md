- Guardian (agent): rules beyond a class's compensating-disarm reservation (Service 3, File 4, Registry 3) are no longer refused outright when a cached or pushed policy arms them back to back, which had left the surplus rules unenforced until the next policy push. A congestion refusal at arm dispatch (`compensating-disarm reservation exhausted`, executor `CapacityExhausted`/`CeilingExhausted`) now parks the arm and redrives it when a permit or executor slot frees. A parked arm keeps its existing claim deadline (5 s by default, observed by the heartbeat maintenance pass), so a large enough surplus of slow-arming rules can still expire and recover on the next successful re-apply. The compensating-disarm safety guarantee is unchanged (#5168).
