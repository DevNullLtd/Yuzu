- **Breaking: the last administrator can no longer be deleted or demoted.** Deleting (deactivating) or demoting the last member of a protected administrator population is now refused: the active local `role='admin'` accounts (with RBAC enforcement on or off) and, with enforcement on, the accounts that hold the `Administrator` role grant. `DELETE /api/settings/users/{username}` and `POST /api/settings/users/{username}/role` answer `409` with a toast (audit `last_administrator_blocked`); SCIM deactivation, delete, `active:false` and group-derived demotion answer `409` with `scimType` `mutability` (audit `scim.user.last_administrator_refused`); both are counted on `yuzu_server_rbac_last_admin_guard_refused_total{transport="dashboard"|"scim"}`. Linked credentials are still revoked before a delete, so when the refusal comes after the early check the `409` says the credentials were revoked and the account remains active; an IdP that retries a `409` is not stopped by it. This is a recovery rule, not a proof anyone can sign in. See `docs/user-manual/rbac.md`.
- **SCIM provisioning refuses accounts it must not own.** A username that already holds an `Administrator` grant, or whose deactivated record has an elevated role, is now refused, and a failed create/revive undo is guarded and escalated (`scim.user.compensation_failed`). A SCIM `PATCH`/`PUT` with `active:true` on a deactivated resource whose role is not `user`, or whose username holds an `Administrator` grant, is now `404` (`scim.user.provenance_denied`, counted on `yuzu_scim_provenance_denied_total`) instead of reactivating it; clear an elevated tombstone's role with SQL (see `docs/user-manual/rbac.md`).
- **SIEM and metric-visible changes.** A dashboard role-change store fault was a `500` audited `denied` / `db_failure` and is now a `503` audited `failure` / `store_failure` with no retry hint: clients should not auto-retry it. New `partial` causes: `user.delete` for a late refusal or a failed session wipe, `user.role_change` for a failed session wipe, and `scim.user.last_administrator_refused` for a LATE refusal (an early refusal and a refused group-derived demotion stay `denied`). Every refused SCIM deprovision or group-derived demotion also raises a critical analytics event. The SCIM store-fault answer is `503` with a `Retry-After` header and no body extension (a failed session wipe stays `500`). The `revoked_principals=` list in SCIM deprovision audit details is lossy, because `,`, space, `=`, control bytes and bytes >= 0x80 in a principal become `_`, so two distinct principals can collapse into one token (the exact identity stays in `identity_links`). Inactive accounts follow store and SCIM rules (demoting an inactive admin is allowed, promoting one is refused; the dashboard route answers `404` for an inactive account). Six counters are new or changed: `yuzu_server_rbac_lifecycle_store_failure_total{op}`, `yuzu_scim_compensation_failed_total{outcome}`, `yuzu_scim_group_recompute_skipped_total{cause}`, `yuzu_scim_role_changes_total` and `yuzu_scim_role_change_failures_total` (now seeded at boot), and a refused compensation no longer counts on `yuzu_server_rbac_last_admin_guard_refused_total`.
- **A SCIM Group request that leaves a member's role undone now says so.** A new `scim.group.recompute_incomplete` audit row (`partial`) records what a Group request's role recompute left undone (a refused or failed demotion, a member whose read failed, or promotions skipped after a store failure; every demotion is attempted), and the Group `created`/`updated`/`deleted` success row now carries `recompute=complete` or `recompute=incomplete`. A `PUT`, `PATCH` or `DELETE` Group request that removes members also writes one flushed WARN log line naming up to 20 of them by SCIM ID, before the first role change (a rename that flips the admin-group match writes it too). A re-send of a Group change cannot repair a member who has already left the group (a refused, failed or undetermined demotion for that member is fixed by demoting the account by hand with `POST /api/settings/users/{username}/role` and `{"role":"user"}`; a refused one needs a second active local administrator account first, and the call has to be made as that second administrator). Known limits of the Group role recompute are listed in `docs/user-manual/scim-provisioning.md` and are tracked in #5673.
- **Upgrade note.** Replicas running an older build do not enforce the guard, so drain them during a rolling upgrade. A server with an authentication database but no RBAC store refuses to start.
