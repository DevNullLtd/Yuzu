- **The agent now stamps census-feeding Guardian events with an ordering pair, (`boot_epoch`, `emit_seq`), so a server can order verdicts without trusting the agent clock.** `GuaranteedStateEvent` gains `boot_epoch` and `emit_seq` (fields 24 and 25), assigned when the agent decides the event's queue order, on both the legacy and the Spark emit paths. `boot_epoch` is claimed once at agent boot from a persisted counter and the boot time; `emit_seq` increases within a boot. A server that compares the pair orders by it; one that does not ignores it and keeps ordering by the timestamp. Events replayed from the lifecycle journal, DEX observations, and events from an agent whose epoch claim failed carry no pair (0/0) and are ordered by the timestamp. The known cases where two boots or two processes can share an epoch are listed under Known limits in the Guardian design document, section 7.1. No operator action is needed: the change is additive and mixed fleets are safe in both directions. (#5690, #5674)
