- **Guardian pages are restyled onto the slate design, and the shared styles other pages reuse move with them.** The Guardian overview, the guard and baseline detail pages and the guard and baseline forms move onto the new panels, a bordered summary readout row, tables, pills, underlined tabs and compact buttons. The shared table, filter-chip, card, pill and button styles are the same ones the DEX, Hardware, Software and Network pages use, so those pages pick up the new look too. A guard's state now reads "Enabled" or "Disabled" instead of "ENABLED" or "DISABLED", and other labels that the old styles forced into capitals now show in normal case (event types appear as stored, for example drift_detected). The "New Guard" action and the form submit buttons are solid primary buttons, and the warning symbols, the refresh arrow and the icons beside section titles are removed. No control is added or removed, and URLs and behaviour are unchanged.
