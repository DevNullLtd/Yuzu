- **A confined operator can no longer list every issued certificate.** `GET /api/v1/ca/issued` without a filter, the MCP tool `list_issued_certs` without filter arguments, and the list in the Settings certificate panel returned every certificate's subject, SAN and issuing identity to any caller holding `Security:Read`, including a management-group-confined one (the seeded Viewer role holds it). They now apply the check the filtered form already used: a confined caller gets `403` (MCP: permission denied) with no permission named, and the panel shows a note instead of the list; if the check reports itself unavailable the answer is `503`, and a read failure partway through the check reads as no Inventory grant and is not treated as confined. Both forms are now audited `crypto.ca.certificates.view` (target `inventory`), set-and-proceed like the filtered form. The Settings panel itself is still not audited.
