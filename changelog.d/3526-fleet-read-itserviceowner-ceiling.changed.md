- **Breaking for service-scoped tokens: `require_fleet_read` now applies the `ITServiceOwner` authority ceiling.** Two effects. (1) `GET /api/v1/enrollment/pending-agents` now answers `403` to a service-scoped token under the seeded defaults: `ITServiceOwner` does not hold `Enrollment:Read` (deliberately not granted), where a service token used to receive the minter's pending-agent view narrowed to its tagged agents; use an Administrator-minted non-service token for that route. (2) Any `ITServiceOwner` revocation (`RbacStore::remove_permission`, recorded in `revoked_seed_defaults`) now refuses service-scoped tokens on every route gated on that pair through the fleet-read gate, whatever their minter holds; re-grant the pair to `ITServiceOwner` to restore access. Every route on the gate (the executions drawer's `/detail` fragment, `GET /api/v1/executions` and its twins, `/api/v1/events`, the legacy `/api/executions*` routes, and the other `Read` routes on that gate) makes the same check `require_permission` already made, through one shared helper. A definitive deny answers `403` with audit `auth.fleet_read_required` / `denied`; a FAILED permission read answers a retryable `503` with `retry_after_ms` 5000 (and bumps `yuzu_server_rbac_read_degrade_total`) on this gate, while `require_permission` and `require_scoped_permission` keep answering `403` for the same failure. With the seeded defaults nothing else changes for `Execution`, `Response`, `Inventory`, `Infrastructure`, `Policy`, `GuaranteedState` and `Workflow:Read`. `kServiceScopeGlobalSafe` is not widened.
