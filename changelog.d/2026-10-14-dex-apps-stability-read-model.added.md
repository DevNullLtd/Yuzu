- **DEX Apps list: per application and per version stability.** `GET /api/v1/dex/apps` and the MCP tool `list_dex_apps` now carry, for each application and for up to 20 of its versions, crashes, hangs, devices affected, `distinct_devices_online`, the share of the Windows devices online now that are affected (95 percent interval), their events per 1,000 online devices (exact interval) and the transparent stability score with its decomposition, plus the population and its basis, `apps_total` (every application with a Windows crash or hang in the window) and `apps_truncated` (the list keeps the 100 applications affecting the most devices). No device floor applies, so figures are published at any device count. The share, rate and score count only affected devices that are online now (the same set the population counts); the crash, hang and device counts stay window-wide. A failed read now answers `503` (MCP: internal error with a retry hint) instead of an empty list, and so does a read over the four-at-once budget. The five pre-existing keys and the list membership are now Windows-scoped (the old read counted every platform): an application with only Linux or macOS signals is no longer listed and mixed applications show Windows counts; the Apps tab, the Overview top apps and `GET /api/v1/dex/app` still count every platform until the tab is rewired. Additive otherwise.
