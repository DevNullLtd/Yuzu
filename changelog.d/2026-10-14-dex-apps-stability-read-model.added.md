- **DEX Apps list: per application and per version stability.** `GET /api/v1/dex/apps` and the MCP tool `list_dex_apps` now carry, for each application and for up to 20 of its versions, crashes, hangs, devices affected, the share of the Windows reporting population affected (95 percent interval), events per 1,000 reporting devices (exact interval) and the transparent stability score with its decomposition, plus the population and its basis, `apps_total` (every application with a Windows crash or hang in the window) and `apps_truncated` (the list keeps the 100 applications affecting the most devices). No device floor applies, so figures are published at any device count. A failed read now answers `503` (MCP: internal error with a retry hint) instead of an empty list. The five pre-existing keys and the list membership are now Windows-scoped (the old read counted every platform): an application with only Linux or macOS signals is no longer listed and mixed applications show Windows counts; the Apps tab, the Overview top apps and `GET /api/v1/dex/app` still count every platform until the tab is rewired. Additive otherwise.
