- **DEX Apps list: per application and per version stability.** `GET /api/v1/dex/apps` and the MCP tool `list_dex_apps` now carry, for each application and for up to 20 of its versions, crashes, hangs, devices affected, the share of the Windows reporting population affected (95 percent interval), events per 1,000 reporting devices (exact interval) and the transparent stability score with its decomposition, plus the population and its basis. No device floor applies, so figures are published at any device count. A failed read now answers `503` (MCP: internal error with a retry hint) instead of an empty list. Additive otherwise; existing fields are unchanged.
