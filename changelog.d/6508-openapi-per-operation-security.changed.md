- **The OpenAPI document no longer tells a client to send an operator token to nine routes that do not accept one.** `POST /elevate`, `POST /elevate/revoke`, `POST /users/me/password` and `POST /users/{username}/password` act on the caller's own browser session and now declare `security: [{cookieAuth}]`. The five `/uploads` routes authenticate by a grant or session credential and now declare the new `uploadGrantAuth` (`X-Yuzu-Upload-Grant`, on `POST /uploads`) or `uploadSessionAuth` (`X-Yuzu-Upload-Session`, on the other four) scheme; their header parameters are unchanged. The `info.description` sentence that named only `GET /ca/root` and `GET /ca/crl` as exceptions names all of these. No handler behaviour changed.
