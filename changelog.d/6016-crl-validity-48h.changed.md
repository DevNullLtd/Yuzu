- **The internal CA's CRL is now valid for 48 hours instead of 7 days (#6016).** `GET /api/v1/ca/crl` serves a CRL whose `nextUpdate` is 48 hours after it was built; the leader's freshness pass still rebuilds it when less than 24 hours remain, so a fleet with no revocations now gets a new CRL about once a day instead of about once every six days (one extra `ca_crl_versions` row per day). A consumer that refuses a CRL past `nextUpdate` must refresh at least daily; revocation still republishes immediately. Server restart only: no agent or gateway change and no migration.
