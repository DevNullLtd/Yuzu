- **Home is the sign-in landing page.** `/home` now renders without a sidebar or header bar: a headline, the sign-in panel, and the fifteen screens of the product as colour-coded tiles in five families, each saying what the area does. It is the one sign-in form (`GET /login` redirects to it; the SSO link appears only when an identity provider is configured and the two-factor note only under MFA enforcement). Signing in keeps the user on `/home`, where a signed-in panel shows the name, role, an "Open Dashboard" button and "Sign out". A page requested without a session is now redirected to `/home?next=<screen key>` instead of `/login`; the page says "Sign in to open <area>." and, after sign-in, offers "Open <area>" rather than redirecting automatically. The old Home worklist (readout, Needs attention, policies, activity, health panels) is gone from `/home`; those panels belong to the Dashboard.
