- **Audit and reviews page, audit log (`/access/audit`).** The audit trail is now readable from the dashboard under Access: exact Principal and Action filters, a last-hour / 24-hour / 7-day window, 50-row pages with a stated 10,000-row paging limit, and a row detail with the event id and full detail text. It shows the same fields as `GET /api/v1/audit` and nothing more (no source address, no principal role), and an unreadable audit store is shown as a red box, never an empty log. The page also offers a user-initiated authentication sample (each draw is recorded as `audit.auth_sample.exported`) and, on request, the audit retention period the store actually enforces ("Never" when nothing is cleaned up). The audit-log manual now describes the trail as access-controlled and retention-managed rather than tamper-evident, because no hash chain or signature backs the log.
