- **Echo: a starved dictionary sweep no longer retries every maintenance pass, an agent stop no longer waits out a dictionary sweep, and a busy purge checkpoint is reported.** A weekly dictionary sweep whose scan did not finish inside its deadline was retried at every 15-minute maintenance pass, each time holding the database lock for the full 2 seconds; it is now retried a day later with the deadline doubled (2, 4, then 8 seconds), and `status` shows the new `dict_gc_last_attempt_ts` and `dict_gc_starved_streak` beside `dict_gc_last_ts`. A dictionary sweep, whether the weekly one or the one an `echo.purge_source` runs under its 60 second limit, now reads the agent's stop signal and ends at its next check once a stop is requested, instead of the stop waiting behind it (the row delete, a commit and the wait for the database lock are not interruptible, and a lost server connection is not a stop). A sweep a stop ended is reported pending but not counted as starved. The purge reply gains `wal_checkpoint_pending`, `true` when the final write-ahead-log checkpoint could not complete (or was skipped because the agent was stopping): the rows are deleted but their pages stay in the log until a later checkpoint truncates it, which running the purge again does and reports, and the result status is `CONSTRAINED` with detail `echo:purge_wal_pending` (`echo:purge_dictionary_pending` takes precedence when the dictionary is also pending). `status` also reads each source's row count and oldest bucket as two cheap statements instead of one full index scan.
