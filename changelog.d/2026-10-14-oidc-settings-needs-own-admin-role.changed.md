- **Breaking: changing sign-in provider settings needs your own administrator role.** `POST /api/settings/oidc` and `PUT /api/config/{key}` for the `oidc_*` keys now refuse, with `403`, a session that is administrator only through a temporary elevation. The refusal is audited as `oidc.configure / denied / elevated_session_oidc_change_blocked` and `config.update / denied / elevated_session_oidc_setting_blocked`. An elevated operator can no longer change the sign-in provider or its administrator group; an account whose own role is `admin` (a local account, or an administrator mapped from the identity provider) is unaffected, and every other runtime-config key is still writable under `Infrastructure:Write`.
