- **Breaking: changing sign-in provider settings requires your own administrator role.** `POST /api/settings/oidc` and `PUT /api/config/{key}` for the `oidc_*` keys now refuse, with `403`, any session whose own role is not `admin`, including one that is administrator only through a temporary elevation, and audit the attempt (`oidc.configure` / `config.update`, `denied`, `elevated_session_oidc_change_blocked` / `elevated_session_oidc_setting_blocked`). A local administrator or an administrator mapped from the identity provider is unaffected; other runtime-config keys are unchanged.
