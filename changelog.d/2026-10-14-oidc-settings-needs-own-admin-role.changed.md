- **Breaking: changing sign-in provider settings requires your own administrator role.** `POST /api/settings/oidc` and `PUT /api/config/{key}` for the `oidc_*` keys now refuse, with `403`, any session whose own role is not `admin`: a session that is administrator only through a temporary elevation, a non-administrator account holding `Infrastructure:Write`, and an MCP-tier token. Automation that writes `oidc_*` with a token whose creator is not an `admin` account is affected. The refusal is audited (`oidc.configure` / `config.update`, `denied`). A local administrator or an administrator mapped from the identity provider is unaffected; other runtime-config keys are unchanged.
