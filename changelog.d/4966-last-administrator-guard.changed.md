- **Breaking: the last administrator can no longer be deleted or demoted.** Deleting (deactivating) or demoting the last member of a protected administrator population is now refused: the active local `role='admin'` accounts (with RBAC enforcement on or off) and, with enforcement on, the accounts that hold the `Administrator` role grant. `DELETE /api/settings/users/{username}` and `POST /api/settings/users/{username}/role` answer `409` with a toast (audit `last_administrator_blocked`); SCIM deactivation, delete, `active:false` and group-derived demotion answer `409` with `scimType` `mutability` (audit `scim.user.last_administrator_refused`); both are counted on `yuzu_server_rbac_last_admin_guard_refused_total{transport="dashboard"|"scim"}`. Linked credentials are still revoked before a delete, so when the refusal comes after the early check the `409` says the credentials were revoked and the account remains active; an IdP that retries a `409` is not stopped by it. This is a recovery rule, not a proof anyone can sign in. SCIM provisioning now refuses a username that already holds an `Administrator` grant or whose deactivated record has an elevated role, and a failed create/revive undo is guarded and escalated (`scim.user.compensation_failed`). Replicas running an older build do not enforce the guard, so drain them during a rolling upgrade; a server with an authentication database but no RBAC store refuses to start. See `docs/user-manual/rbac.md`. A SCIM `PATCH`/`PUT` with `active:true` on a deactivated resource whose role is not `user`, or whose username holds an `Administrator` grant, is now `404` (`scim.user.provenance_denied`, counted on `yuzu_scim_provenance_denied_total`) instead of reactivating it; clear an elevated tombstone's role with SQL (see `docs/user-manual/rbac.md`). SIEM-visible changes: a dashboard role-change store fault was a `500` audited `denied` / `db_failure` and is now a `503` audited `failure` / `store_failure` with no retry hint; new `partial` causes (`user.delete` for a late refusal or a failed session wipe, `user.role_change` for a failed session wipe, and `scim.user.last_administrator_refused` for a LATE refusal, while an early refusal and a refused group-derived demotion stay `denied`); a new `scim.group.recompute_incomplete` audit row (`partial`) records what a Group request's role recompute left undone (a refused or failed demotion, a member whose read failed, or promotions skipped after a store failure; every demotion is attempted), and the Group `created`/`updated`/`deleted` success row now carries `recompute=complete` or `recompute=incomplete`; every refused SCIM deprovision or group-derived demotion also raises a critical analytics event; the SCIM store-fault answer is `503` with a `Retry-After` header and no body extension (a failed session wipe stays `500`); the `revoked_principals=` list in SCIM deprovision audit details is lossy, because `,`, space, `=`, control bytes and bytes >= 0x80 in a principal become `_`, so two distinct principals can collapse into one token (the exact identity stays in `identity_links`); inactive accounts follow store and SCIM rules (demoting an inactive admin is allowed, promoting one is refused; the dashboard route answers `404` for an inactive account); and four counters are new or changed (`yuzu_server_rbac_lifecycle_store_failure_total{op}`, `yuzu_scim_compensation_failed_total{outcome}`, `yuzu_scim_group_recompute_skipped_total{direction}`, and a refused compensation no longer counts on `yuzu_server_rbac_last_admin_guard_refused_total`). Known limits of the Group role recompute are listed in `docs/user-manual/scim-provisioning.md` and are scheduled to be removed by a follow-up change.
