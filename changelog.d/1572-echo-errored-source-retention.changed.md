- **Echo now ages out the rows of a source whose enable flag is `errored` and was never paused.** Collection still stops for such a source, but its rows used to be held past their retention for ever; they now follow the normal retention ceiling and byte-budget eviction, and the agent logs a warning on each maintenance pass while a source stays errored and unpaused. Rows of a source an operator paused, or that was paused when its flag went bad, are still preserved, as are those whose `<source>_paused_at` marker is unreadable or malformed.
