- **Breaking - Echo page reads the hourly tables: upgrade agents before the server.** The process tree is built per hour from the host's hourly roll-up: pick a window with the **Last hour**, **Last 24 hours**, **Last 7 days** or custom presets, then choose the hour to show. Nodes are process names with started and stopped counts for the hour, and the **Anomalies only** and text filters stay. The device **DNS** and **ARP** panels show the chosen hour's appear and remove counts, and the capture sources are cards with **Hourly rows**, **Oldest bucket** and the tiers each source is kept in. A device whose agent has no roll-up store shows "upgrade the agent" in place of data, and an upgraded agent behind an older server shows empty panels (see "Behaviour change: the Echo page reads the roll-up tables" in the upgrade guide). What the page no longer shows: process ids, command lines and paths, running or exited state, the **On boot** and **On agent install** anchors, and the **Exact** retention column.
- **Retention-paused sources and Settings use the hourly tables.** The retention-paused list shows **Hourly rows** and **Oldest hourly bucket** and says a paused source is never pruned. Settings > Data and retention drops the Exact column, shows the device byte budget and marks the hourly tier as budget-dependent.
- **Timeline shows roll-up states.** Every stretch of a lane is drawn as rolled up at the hourly, daily or monthly tier, pruned, paused or not readable. The "what is kept" chart starts with the hourly band, marks its reach as budget-dependent and states how far back it reaches on the device.
