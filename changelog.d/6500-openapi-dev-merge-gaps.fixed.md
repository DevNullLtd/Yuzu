- **The served OpenAPI document now describes the `/config` and `/dex/score-policy` write routes as they behave.** Score-policy `PUT` and `DELETE` declare `411` (unmeasurable body) and `413` (body over 320 KiB), and their `200` declares the `Sec-Audit-Failed` header. `PUT /config/{key}` completes its `401` and `503` text and names the service-scoped refusal on every key together with the audit row it writes. `GET /config` declares its `401`. The `/config` errors reference the standard error envelope, which now says that a step-up refusal carries no `retry_after_ms`. The DEX guide covers the customer score policy.
