- **The served OpenAPI document now describes the `/config` and `/dex/score-policy` write routes as they behave.** Score-policy `PUT` and `DELETE` declare `411` (unmeasurable body) and `413` (body over 320 KiB), and their `200` declares the `Sec-Audit-Failed` header, as the `200` and `503` of `PUT /config/{key}` do. The score-policy `200` schema requires `data` and `meta`, and the text says when a retry with the old `If-Match` answers `200` instead of `409`. `PUT /config/{key}` completes its `401` and `503` text and names the service-scoped refusal on every key together with the audit row it writes. `GET /config` declares its `401`. The `/config` errors reference the standard error envelope, which now says that a step-up refusal carries no `retry_after_ms`. The served DEX guide covers the customer score policy. The recovery hint of an unknown-outcome score-policy write now says that a revision equal to your `If-Match` means the policy is as it was before your request, instead of claiming the write did not apply. The `400` that `PUT /config/dex_score_policy` answers now says the key is not written through that route and names `PUT /api/v1/dex/score-policy` and the MCP tool `set_dex_score_policy`. (#6500)
