- **Breaking — policies that previously dispatched gated or destructive instructions are now blocked.** Policy evaluation now applies the action approval requirement before it dispatches; earlier builds dispatched an execute-gated, destructive or system-reserved pair without applying the approval requirement. A policy whose check, fix or post-fix check names an action with an execute gate, a destructive or reserved action, or one the server cannot classify is not dispatched. Its devices show a new `blocked` status, which counts as failing in the compliance pill and readout and in the home tile (which now also counts `error`). `POST /api/policies/{id}/evaluate` and `/remediate` answer `409` with the reason, and creating a fragment or a policy, or enabling a policy, with such an instruction answers `400`. Product pack install is not refused: a pack's policy installs and then shows `blocked`. New: `yuzu_server_policy_dispatch_denied_total{reason}` and a `policy.dispatch` audit row (written when a refusal changes a stored status or the status write fails, and on every operator-triggered refusal). See "Action approval and the `blocked` status" in the policy engine manual.
