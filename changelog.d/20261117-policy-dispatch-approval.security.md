- **Breaking, fresh deployment only: a policy that names a gated or destructive instruction is blocked.** Policy evaluation applies the action approval requirement before it dispatches. A policy whose check, fix or post-fix check names an action with an execute gate, a destructive or reserved action, or one the server cannot classify is not dispatched. Its devices show a new `blocked` status, which counts as failing in the compliance pill and readout and in the home tile (which now also counts `error`). `POST /api/policies/{id}/evaluate` and `/remediate` answer `409` with the reason, and creating a fragment or a policy, or enabling a policy, with such an instruction answers `400`. Most remediation policies are affected: nothing can approve a policy to run an action that has an execute gate, and the only mutating actions that remain usable as a policy fix are `tags.set`, `tags.delete`, `agent_actions.set_log_level` and `chargen.chargen_stop`; a remediation attempt on any other policy's fix is refused and its devices show `blocked` until the next check. A pair the server cannot classify (a custom or third-party plugin, or an instruction with an empty action) is not sent either, and its devices show `blocked`, which counts as failing. Product pack install is not refused: a pack's policy installs and then shows `blocked`. New: `yuzu_server_policy_dispatch_denied_total{reason}` and a `policy.dispatch` audit row (written when a refusal changes a stored status or the status write fails, and on every operator-triggered refusal). See "Action approval and the `blocked` status" in the policy engine manual.
