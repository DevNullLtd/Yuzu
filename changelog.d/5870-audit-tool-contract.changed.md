- **Breaking - `query_audit_log` publishes its contract and the legacy audit route validates its filters.** The MCP tool's string filters declare `maxLength` (1024 characters) and are refused above it with `-32602`; every parameter has a description; the output declares and always returns `has_more` (true when more matching rows exist past the page; a short page is the last one); and its refusals and store faults are A4 errors: a bad filter carries a remediation and no retry hint, a store fault carries `retry_after_ms`. The legacy `GET /api/audit` and `GET /api/v1/audit` now refuse a NUL or invalid UTF-8 in a string filter with `400`, and the legacy route also refuses a negative `since`/`until` and an inverted window, as the v1 and MCP twins do. Callers that sent such values were previously answered (the NUL silently widened the filter).
