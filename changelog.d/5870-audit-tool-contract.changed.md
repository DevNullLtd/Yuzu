- **`query_audit_log` publishes its contract.** The MCP tool's string filters declare `maxLength` (256, `target_id` 512) and are refused above it, every parameter has a description, the output declares and always returns `has_more` (true when more matching rows exist past the page; a short page is the last one), and its refusals and store faults are A4 errors: a bad filter carries a remediation and no retry hint, a store fault carries `retry_after_ms`. The legacy `GET /api/audit` now refuses a NUL in a filter, a negative `since`/`until` and an inverted window with `400`, as the v1 and MCP twins do.
