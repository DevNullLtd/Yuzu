- **System pages: Load generator start and stop.** On **Settings > Developer and API** a Load generator panel starts and stops the generator on every connected device the caller can see. Start asks for confirmation. Every control calls a route that already existed and keeps that route's permission, audit and bounds; a refusal, such as a missing permission or the approval requirement, is shown as the server worded it. The devices-online figure is informational and does not enable or disable either button.
