- **`GET /api/v1/dex/apps` and `list_dex_apps` carry `observer_state`, `policy.interval_confidence` and `score.tier`.** `observer_state` is the live count, per platform in scope, of online devices whose crash and hang observer is armed, not armed (`disarmed`) or not reporting (`none_reported`), or `null` when no observer source is wired; it is the live counterpart of the shipped-design `crash_source_coverage`. `policy.interval_confidence` is the confidence level of every interval. `score.tier` is the display name each score reads as under the policy that scored the row (an override's own names for an overridden application and its versions), `null` when the score is withheld; the page prints the same value. The OpenAPI schema, the MCP output schema and the REST and MCP descriptions say so, and the `DexAppPerfInterval.reliable` and `withheld` descriptions are corrected.
