- **Sample alert rule for CRL source registrations refused while name lookups are backing up.** The new `yuzu-crl-source` group of `docs/prometheus/yuzu-alerts.yml` adds `YuzuCrlSourceResolverBusy`, a warning when `yuzu_server_revocation_source_degrade_total{reason="dns_busy"}` increased in the last 15 minutes, with promtool cases.
