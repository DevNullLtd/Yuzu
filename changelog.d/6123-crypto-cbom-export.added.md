- **`/crypto` CBOM export: the crypto-library inventory as a CycloneDX 1.7 bill of materials.** New `GET /api/v1/crypto/cbom` (optional `family`, `ecosystem`) and MCP tool `export_crypto_cbom` export a CycloneDX 1.7 bill of the cryptographic-library packages Yuzu sees installed (library components only: it does not yet describe algorithms, certificates, keys or protocols) as JSON that CBOM/SBOM tooling can ingest, re-shaping the family summary the `/crypto` page already shows, behind the same `Inventory:Read` gate, confinement and read-capacity bound as `GET /api/v1/crypto/libraries`. One `library` component per (family, version, ecosystem) bucket, with Yuzu's facts under `yuzu:crypto:` properties; no `cryptoProperties`, strength or good/bad field (interpretation stays out of core), and no `purl`, `cpe` or hash (vulnerability scanners will not match these components; an empty scan result means "not matchable", not "no known vulnerabilities"). A cut version list is never silent: it is flagged on the affected components and listed in `yuzu:crypto:truncated_families`. The response is `application/vnd.cyclonedx+json; version=1.7` with `Cache-Control: no-store, private`, a fresh random `serialNumber` per export, a `yuzu:crypto:scope` of `fleet` or `confined` and a top-level `compositions` of `incomplete`, and is audited as `crypto.cbom.export` (`mcp.export_crypto_cbom`) with the serial, ecosystem and component count in the detail. The export is validated in CI against the vendored official `bom-1.7` schema.
