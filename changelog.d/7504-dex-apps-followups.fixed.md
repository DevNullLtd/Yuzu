- **DEX Scoring and Alerts keep the window you chose, DEX chips, tabs and links work from the keyboard, a late DEX response no longer replaces a newer view, and bidirectional and zero-width characters in names are shown.** The Scoring and Alerts sub-navigation now carries the selected window instead of resetting it to 7 days. Filter chips, sub-navigation tabs and text links on the DEX pages are native buttons: they take focus, open with Enter and show a focus ring, and look the same as before. Every control that swaps the DEX pane cancels a request still in flight for it, so a slow Apps response can no longer overwrite the Overview you moved to. A name on the DEX pages that contains a bidirectional control or a zero-width character prints it as visible text (`<U+202E>`) instead of acting on it; tooltips and form values keep their exact bytes, and the device, inventory, software and Echo pages are not covered yet. The versions expander on the Apps page exposes one visually hidden sentence when opened, so it is never empty to a screen reader.
