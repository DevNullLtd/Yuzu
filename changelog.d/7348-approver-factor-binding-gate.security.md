- **An MFA step-up proof now counts only for the factor it was earned with (#6079).** Before this change the central step-up check and JIT elevation compared a session's stored proof with nothing about the account's factor, so a proof held by a session that outlived a factor change kept counting (a failed or raced session delete, or a factor cleared outside the application); the approver rule compared only the enrolment instant, which a repeated or skewed instant could defeat. One judge (`judge_local_mfa_binding`) now compares the factor generation stored with the proof against the account's current one on the 39 ordinary step-up routes while `--mfa-step-up-window-secs` is above 0, on the 2 factor-credential routes, on the 3 JIT-elevation routes and for approve and reject. `POST /api/settings/mfa/recovery-codes` and `POST /api/settings/mfa/disable` accept a local session only. The breaking details are in the entries under **Changed**.
