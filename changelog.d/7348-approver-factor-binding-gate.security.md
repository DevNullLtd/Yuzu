- **An MFA step-up proof now counts only for the factor it was earned with (#6079).** Before this change a proof earned against one factor kept counting after the factor was replaced: disabling MFA and enrolling again, or an administrator clearing the factor with `yuzu-server --mfa-reset`, left earlier proofs valid, including for approving or rejecting a request. One judge (`judge_local_mfa_binding`) now compares the factor generation stored with the proof against the account's current one on the 41 step-up routes (approve and reject among them) and the 3 JIT-elevation routes. `POST /api/settings/mfa/recovery-codes` and `POST /api/settings/mfa/disable` accept a local session only. The tightening is the minimum that closes that gap; the breaking details are in the entries under **Changed**.
