- **A blocked stderr can no longer stall agent log delivery (#5023).** When a log sink failed (a full disk, no free file descriptors, a broken pipe) at the same moment the agent's stderr was itself blocked (for example a full pipe to a stalled log collector), the agent's single logging worker thread could block writing its diagnostic line and stop delivering every later log line until the process was restarted. The diagnostic line is now written by a short-lived helper thread, at most one at a time; while one is stuck, further diagnostic lines are dropped rather than blocking delivery. No flag or format change.
