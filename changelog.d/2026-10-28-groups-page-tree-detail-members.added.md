- **Groups and enrolment page shows management groups.** The management-groups panel on `/groups` now lists the group tree with each group's type and member count, and selecting a group shows its details (parent, description, creator, dates and the stored rule of a rule-based group) and its members. The panels are read-only; groups are still created and deleted in Settings. A member list shows only the members within the viewer's scope and states that without revealing how many were left out. A group read that fails shows a "could not be read" box with Retry instead of an empty tree or "0 members". The tree draws at most 500 groups and a member list at most 200 rows, with a note when more exist.
