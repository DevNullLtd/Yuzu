- **The fenced leader epoch now carries the Postgres timeline, closing the lossy-promotion hole (#6535).** The epoch is minted as `(timeline << 32) + nextval(leader_epoch_seq)`. Under asynchronous replication, or a degraded non-strict quorum, a promoted standby could lack the latest election and re-mint the epoch a paused former leader still held, so the epoch fence admitted both; every promotion starts a new timeline, so the new leader's epoch is now always higher. Visible effects: `yuzu_server_leader_epoch` and `leader_state.current_leader_epoch` read at least 4294967297 after the upgrade, and the `leader_epoch_seq` sequence is capped at 4294967295 by an automatic migration. The elector now refuses to open if it cannot read the timeline. Not covered: two live primaries, a physical-snapshot restore without archive recovery, a logical restore, a rolling upgrade while an older server can still lead, and a downgrade (see `docs/user-manual/upgrading.md`, which also gives the one-line reset for `plugin_config_store.enablement_meta.issuer_epoch`). The safe-to-scale gate stays closed.
