- **Adds a fleet application-usage read model and a database query owner behind the Digital Employee Experience Apps list; not yet wired to any surface.** For each application it totals the devices that ran it, run seconds and launches over a 30-day basis, and divides crashes and hangs on those same devices by the hours of use to give a rate per hour of use, marked as estimated from 30-day usage. Figures can be sliced by platform, management group or tag key, with cells below a device floor merged into an "other" cell (the floor defaults to one device), and a name shared by two applications is withheld. Nothing is exposed on the REST API, MCP or the dashboard yet, and per-device usage keeps its forensic permission.
