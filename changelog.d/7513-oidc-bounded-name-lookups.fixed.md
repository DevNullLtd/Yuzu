- **OIDC name lookups stop waiting at their bound.** On a glibc build the OIDC name lookups could wait about 30 s past the intended 10 s bound; they now stop waiting at the bound. The lookups run on a worker with an OIDC-owned pool of 16 slots, and a lookup that outlives its bound keeps its slot until the resolver returns. When all 16 slots are held, or a worker cannot be started, a new lookup is refused at once with "the host name lookup was not started" (a timeout or a resolver failure still reads "the host name did not resolve"). That refusal is not remembered: discovery does not wait out its 30 s no-retry window for it, and the NAT64 prefix lookup is retried on the next fetch. A NAT64 prefix lookup that times out or fails is still remembered as failed for 1 minute; on a DNS64 network set `--oidc-nat64-prefix`. New gauge `yuzu_server_oidc_resolver_in_flight` (fed only on POSIX builds, 0 elsewhere); see the upgrading guide.
