- **A policy whose check instruction was deleted writes its per-device error verdicts in one batched statement.** The evaluator thread no longer makes one database round trip per target (about 25 seconds at 5,000 targets, far longer under a slow pool). A failed chunk is retried on fresh pool leases for a connection-class failure or a pool lease not obtained within 3 s (at most the pool size plus one attempts per chunk, the first included; a statement error is not retried). No new attempt starts after 30 s from the start of the call, so chunks not reached are counted as failed. A chunk that fails does not stop the next one while the budget lasts. A call that lost at least one chunk is counted once in `yuzu_server_policy_eval_errors_total{phase="error_verdict_write"}` (retries in `phase="error_verdict_retry"`). The gauge `yuzu_server_policy_error_verdict_unwritten_agents` is above zero on the server process that lost the write until that policy next writes a verdict on that server (an entry ages out after 24 hours and is not kept across a restart). `YuzuPolicyErrorVerdictWriteFailed` (critical) fires when the gauge stays above zero for 5 minutes, and `YuzuPolicyErrorVerdictWriteRetrying` warns when `error_verdict_retry` keeps increasing for ten minutes.
