- **A policy whose check instruction was deleted writes its per-device error verdicts in one batched statement.** The evaluator thread no longer makes one database round trip per target (about 25 seconds at 5,000 targets, far longer under a slow pool). A failed chunk is retried on fresh pool leases for a connection-class failure only (up to the pool size plus one attempts, inside one 30 s budget for the whole write; a statement error is not retried), and one that still fails does not stop the rest: every remaining chunk is still written, and the failure is counted in `yuzu_server_policy_eval_errors_total{phase="error_verdict_write"}`.
