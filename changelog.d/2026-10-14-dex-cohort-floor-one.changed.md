- **DEX cohort floor is now one device.** The statistical floor every DEX app-performance and cohort read derives from (`kDexCohortFloor`) is 1 instead of 10: a `(version, day)` point, a cohort row or a per-version figure is published with its exact statistics whenever at least one device contributed, and `suppressed` is now true only for a point or cohort no device contributed to. This covers the fleet, management-group and device-tag app-performance trends (`GET /api/v1/dex/perf/{app,group,tag}`, their MCP twins and the dashboard fragments), the fleet cohort benchmarks and head-to-head diff (`/perf/cohorts`, `/perf/cohort-diff`), the per-device cohort comparison strip, and the per-application stability figures. The `floor` field those responses echo now reads 1; `suppressed` and `small_cohort` stay on the wire (`small_cohort` on `/perf/compare` can no longer be true); nothing is removed. Single-device points are published as fleet-wide aggregates by application, version or cohort value only — no `agent_id` or device identifier is emitted, and every read stays behind `GuaranteedState:Read`.
