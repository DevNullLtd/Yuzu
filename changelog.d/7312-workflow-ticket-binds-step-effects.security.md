- **A supervised-tier approval ticket for `execute_workflow` binds the ordered `plugin.action`, approval mode and concurrency mode of every step, and every step pair is authorized before anything is sent.** The ticket used to cover only the workflow id and the agent list, while the stored instruction definitions behind its steps stay editable, so a step repointed between approval and re-call could run under the old approval. The ticket's subject now leads with the first three `plugin.action` values and a SHA-256 over the ordered step tuples, and the re-call recomputes it: a repoint is refused `-32003` `definition_changed` with the ticket unspent, and one landing after that check runs nothing for the affected step. Separately, `prepare` asks the dispatch chokepoint's dry run once per distinct step `plugin.action` with the caller's real identity before the `requested` row or any send: a caller-level denial (RBAC, the admin-or-approval gate with no approval, the all-OS kill switch) refuses the whole call up front as a non-retryable `403` `dispatch_denied` instead of surfacing mid-run after earlier steps were sent. Approval tickets for `execute_workflow` do not exist before this release, so no pending ticket is affected.
