- **Agent and policy identifiers are encoded in page links.** Identifiers used in dashboard links and actions (pending agents, compliance policies and fragments, update packages, device links, results sort links) are percent-encoded, and an identifier that is empty, `.` or `..` is no longer offered as an action. New agent registrations must use letters, digits, `.`, `_`, `:` and `-`, and new policy and fragment identifiers letters, digits, `.`, `_` and `-` (up to 128 characters); agents and policies that already exist are not rejected. See the upgrade notes.
