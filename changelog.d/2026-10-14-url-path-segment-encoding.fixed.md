- **Agent and policy identifiers are encoded in page links.** Identifiers shown in dashboard links and actions (pending agents, compliance policies and fragments, update packages, device links, results sort links) are percent-encoded, and unusual identifiers are no longer offered as actions. New agent registrations must use letters, digits, `.`, `_`, `:` and `-`; agents and policies that already exist keep working. New policy and fragment identifiers are limited to letters, digits, `.`, `_` and `-` (up to 128 characters).
