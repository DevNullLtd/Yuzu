- **The DEX Apps tab now shows application stability.** Per application and per version it shows devices affected, the share of the online population with its interval, the rate per 1,000 online devices, the stability score with its decomposition, the policy in force and a platform filter (default all platforms), all from the same model as `GET /api/v1/dex/apps`. A failed read is an error panel with no population or policy line, and every withheld or unknown figure says why in words.
