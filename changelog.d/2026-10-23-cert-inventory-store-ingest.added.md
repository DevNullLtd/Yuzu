- **Fleet certificate inventory store and ingest (server side).** The server now persists the `certificate_inventory` daily-sync source in a new Postgres schema, `cert_inventory`, on both the direct `ReportInventory` and the gateway `ProxyInventory` paths through one shared ingest seam. Per-store replace rules keep a partly read or unreadable store's rows instead of ageing them away; a hash-skip refreshes only what the last full report carried; a record over a cap is refused, never truncated, and invalid UTF-8 is replaced and marked. The store joins the agent-decommission cascade (now eight stores) and `/readyz`; decommissioning a device records a tombstone so a still-running agent's later reports are acknowledged, counted (`yuzu_server_cert_inventory_ingest_total{result,reason}`, `reason="decommissioned"`) and dropped. There is no read surface and no agent collector in this change.
