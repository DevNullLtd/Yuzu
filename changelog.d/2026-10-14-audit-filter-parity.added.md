- **Audit read parity across REST v1, MCP and the legacy route.** `GET /api/v1/audit` now accepts `target_type`, `target_id`, `since`, `until` and `offset`, returns each row's `id`, and reports the real `start` and `page_size` in `pagination`. The MCP `query_audit_log` tool gains `target_id` and `offset`. `target_id` requires `target_type`; malformed or out-of-range values are rejected with an error rather than ignored. Rows with equal timestamps are now ordered by `id`, so paging never repeats or skips a row. MCP `query_audit_log` callers that sent a non-string `principal`, `action` or `target_type`, or a non-integer or negative `since`/`until`, previously got an unfiltered read; they now get an invalid-params error (-32602).
