- **Breaking: the legacy `GET /api/responses/{id}/export` writes a fail-closed success audit row (#4644).**
  A bulk read of up to 10,000 rows or 50 MiB left no trace unless a management-group scope drop occurred; it now emits `response.read` with `result=success`, `target_type=Execution`, `target_id=<instruction_id>` and `detail=legacy response export cid=<correlation_id>` on every served export (CSV and JSON), after the store read and before any body is built, using the same verb as the v1 export so the two are countable together. Like the v1 twin it is fail-closed: when the row cannot be persisted, or the audit pipeline throws, the route answers `503` (A4 envelope, `retry_after_ms: 5000`, `Sec-Audit-Failed: true`) and serves no data, and the scope-drop `denied` row on that route is fail-closed too. Scripted consumers must retry on `503`. The legacy list and aggregate routes are unchanged.
