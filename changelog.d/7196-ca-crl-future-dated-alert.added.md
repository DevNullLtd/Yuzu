- **New alert `YuzuCaCrlFutureDated` catches an internal-CA CRL dated in the future (#7097).** It fires (warning) when `yuzu_server_ca_crl_published_timestamp_seconds` (the publishing replica's clock at build) stays more than an hour ahead of the monitor's clock for 2 minutes. In practice that means a replica published with its clock more than about 62 minutes ahead of real time; every CRL a gateway refuses (a gateway tolerates a `thisUpdate` up to an hour ahead and refuses beyond that) alerts within about two minutes, and the two expiry alerts stay silent. The runbook section "CRL dated in the future" in `docs/pki-architecture.md` gives the fix: check every replica's clock and correct the wrong one, then restart one replica with a correct clock, because the freshness pass does not replace a future-dated CRL until less than 24 hours of its `nextUpdate` remain. The SOC 2 evidence document now maps the four CRL alerts (publish failing, near expiry, expired, future-dated) to CC7.2, supporting CC6.3, and states what an auditor can and cannot retrieve (#7099). A new rig script, `scripts/e2e-crl-freshness-test.sh`, and two TSan-tagged tests cover the freshness gauges (#7098).
