- **`execution_artifacts` scratch sweep: clock-step guard and rotating start.** The stale-scratch-directory sweep skips its passes for one hour of monotonic time after the wall clock steps forward relative to the process's monotonic clock between two passes, including the restoration after a backward step that lands on a sampled pass. This narrows the window in which a freshly created directory can look an hour old during a concurrent dispatch's create-to-open window, and sweeping resumes without a restart. The guard compares the two sampled endpoints only, so a backward excursion that begins and ends between two passes, or cumulative sub-tolerance drift, is not detected; that case fails closed and a re-dispatch succeeds. Each pass also starts at a rotating position in the enumeration, which reduces but does not eliminate the chance that a run of persistently failing entries delays a removable orphan: the counter is per agent process and resets on restart, and rotation covers only the first 4096 root entries (a larger directory reports `deferred`). The sweep's caps are now injectable and covered by Windows-leg unit tests (#4503, #4504, #4505).
