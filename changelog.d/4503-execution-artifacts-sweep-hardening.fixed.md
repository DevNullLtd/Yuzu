- **`execution_artifacts` scratch sweep: clock-step guard and no permanent starvation.** The stale-scratch-directory sweep skips its passes for one hour of monotonic time after the wall clock steps forward relative to the process's monotonic clock between two passes — including the restoration after a backward step — so a freshly created directory can no longer look an hour old during a concurrent dispatch's create-to-open window, and sweeping resumes without a restart; it rotates its enumeration start across passes so a run of persistently failing entries cannot hide a removable orphan forever (within the root-entry cap, which is reported as `deferred` when exceeded); and its caps are now injectable so every cap and deadline is unit-tested (#4503, #4504, #4505).
