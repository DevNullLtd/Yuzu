- **`execution_artifacts` scratch sweep: clock-step guard and no permanent starvation.** The stale-scratch-directory sweep skips its passes for one hour of monotonic time after the wall clock steps forward relative to the process's monotonic clock between two passes — including the restoration after a backward step — which narrows the window in which a freshly created directory can look an hour old during a concurrent dispatch's create-to-open window (the guard compares the two sampled endpoints only, so a backward excursion that begins and ends between two passes, or cumulative sub-tolerance drift, is not detected; that case fails closed and a re-dispatch succeeds), and sweeping resumes without a restart; it rotates its enumeration start across passes so a run of persistently failing entries cannot hide a removable orphan forever (within the root-entry cap, which is reported as `deferred` when exceeded); and its caps are now injectable so every cap and deadline is unit-tested (#4503, #4504, #4505).
