- **Breaking: changing an account's role, or granting elevation eligibility, needs your own administrator role.** `POST /api/settings/users/{username}/role` and the `eligible=true` form of `POST /api/v1/users/{username}/elevation-eligibility` now refuse, with `403`, a session that is administrator only through a temporary elevation. The refusal is audited as `user.role_change / denied / elevated_session_role_change_blocked` and `user.elevation_eligibility.set / denied / elevated_session_eligibility_grant_blocked`. An elevated operator can no longer change roles or grant eligibility; an account whose own role is `admin` (a local account, or an administrator mapped from the identity provider) is unaffected, and revoking eligibility is still allowed during an elevation.
