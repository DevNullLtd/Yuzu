- **Inline OpenAPI no longer misdescribes three plugin-config and upload responses.** `GET` and `PUT /plugin-config/{plugin}/{key}` now show the `{data, meta}` envelope the handlers return, `DELETE /plugin-config/{plugin}/{key}/secret` declares its `404` for an absent secret (the attempt is still audited first), and `PUT /uploads/{upload_id}/chunk` declares `500` for a failed open or write instead of folding it into `503`, and no longer implies `size_exceeded` answers `410` (it answers `413`).
