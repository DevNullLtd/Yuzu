- **Inline OpenAPI no longer misdescribes the plugin-config and upload-chunk responses.** `GET` and `PUT /plugin-config/{plugin}/{key}` now show the `{data, meta}` envelope the handlers return. The statuses the handlers answer but the spec omitted are declared: `413` and `500` on both `PUT` writes, `400` and `500` on `DELETE /plugin-config/{plugin}/{key}`, and `404` and `500` on `DELETE /plugin-config/{plugin}/{key}/secret` (an absent secret is still audited first). `PUT /uploads/{upload_id}/chunk` declares `500` for a failed open or write instead of folding it into `503`, names the TLS-required `400`, and no longer implies `size_exceeded` answers `410` (it answers `413`). The same status sets are corrected on the kill-switch `PUT`, `GET /plugin-config`, and the upload status, cancel and commit routes (commit's verification failure is `500`, not `503`), and the secret `PUT` and both `DELETE` success responses now show their real `{data, meta}` and `{deleted, meta}` bodies (#6167).
