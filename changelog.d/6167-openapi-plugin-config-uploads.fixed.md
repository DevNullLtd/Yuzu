- **Inline OpenAPI no longer misdescribes the plugin-config and upload-chunk responses.** `GET` and `PUT /plugin-config/{plugin}/{key}` now show the `{data, meta}` envelope the handlers return. The statuses the handlers answer but the spec omitted are declared: `413` and `500` on both `PUT` writes, `400` and `500` on `DELETE /plugin-config/{plugin}/{key}`, and `404` and `500` on `DELETE /plugin-config/{plugin}/{key}/secret` (an absent secret is still audited first). `PUT /uploads/{upload_id}/chunk` declares `500` for a failed open or write instead of folding it into `503`, names the TLS-required `400`, and no longer implies `size_exceeded` answers `410` (it answers `413`). This is a correction of the served document and of the matching `rest-api.md` and `audit-log.md` text; no route behaviour changed. The manual, the served API description, the Upload Grants guide and the two upload security schemes now also state, as a known defect, that the agent upload routes answer a request carrying only the grant or session header with the generic `401` (#5626), and the audit catalogue says the shipped client therefore produces no device-side upload audit rows. The kill-switch `PUT` `500` text now says how to read the switch back, how to check the audit rows when the read-back cannot show whether the write applied, and when not to repeat the `PUT`; the revoke `503` text says what a `404` on the retry means; and the mint, throwing-audit-hook and detection notes are stated. The same status sets are corrected on the kill-switch `PUT` (adds `413` and `500`), `GET /plugin-config` (adds `400`), and the upload status (adds `400`), cancel (adds `400` and `410`) and commit (adds `410`; its verification failure is `500`, not `503`) routes, and the kill-switch, secret `PUT` and both `DELETE` success responses now show their real `{data, meta}` and `{deleted, meta}` bodies (#6167).
