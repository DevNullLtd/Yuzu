- Settings fragments that show account, role, token, enrolment or access-review data, and the answers of the actions that re-render them (pending agents, MFA, users, enrolment tokens), are now sent with `Cache-Control: no-store, private` and `Vary: Cookie`, so a browser, proxy or back-forward cache cannot replay them to another session or after the permission is withdrawn. The MFA routes keep their `Pragma` and `Referrer-Policy` headers and gain `Vary: Cookie`. (#6940)
