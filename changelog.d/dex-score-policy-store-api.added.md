- **DEX score policy is persisted and named on every Apps payload.** `GET /api/v1/dex/apps` and `list_dex_apps` now carry a `policy` block (source `default`|`custom`, revision, the four tier edges and names, and a `fault` reason when a stored document is invalid and the shipped defaults are in force) and a per-application `profile` marker; the policy lives under the runtime-config key `dex_score_policy`, read by every server instance within 5 seconds, and the generic `PUT /api/config/dex_score_policy` is refused in favour of the policy route.
