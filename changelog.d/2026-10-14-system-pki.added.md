- **System > Certificates and secrets (`/settings/pki`).** A new admin-only page under the Settings sub-navigation, built over routes that already exist. It shows the certificate authority (trust mode, algorithm, validity, fingerprint, CA and CSR downloads, enterprise chain import), the revocation list (published time, next update, revoked count, and whether a revocation is missing from the published list), the issued-certificate inventory with Expired and Revoked states and a purpose-aware revoke confirmation, and a read-only key-encryption-key status panel (`GET /fragments/settings/kek`, same `Security:Read` gate as `GET /api/v1/secrets/kek/status`). Each fragment answers a denied or degraded read with an inline note rather than an empty list. The Settings page and its Internal CA panel are unchanged; the revoke and import-chain buttons on both pages now show a toast when the server refuses them.
- **CRL figures on the certificate inventory.** `GET /api/v1/ca/issued` (`meta`) and the MCP `list_issued_certs` tool now carry `crl_version`, `crl_published_at`, `crl_next_update`, `crl_revoked_count` and `crl_stale`. Each is `null` when it could not be determined (a CRL published before counts were recorded has a `null` count, never `0`), so a client can judge revocation freshness without downloading the DER list.
