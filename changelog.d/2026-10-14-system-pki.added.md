- **System > Certificates and secrets (`/settings/pki`).** A new admin-only page under the Settings sub-navigation, built over existing data and permissions, with no new write route. It shows the certificate authority (trust mode, algorithm, validity, fingerprint, CA and CSR downloads, enterprise chain import), the revocation list (published time, next update, revoked count, and whether a revocation is missing from the published list), the issued-certificate inventory (the newest 500 certificates; older ones are found with `GET /api/v1/ca/issued?offset=` and revoked with `POST /api/v1/ca/revoke`) with Expired and Revoked states and a purpose-aware revoke confirmation, and a read-only key-encryption-key status panel (`GET /fragments/settings/kek`, same `Security:Read` gate as `GET /api/v1/secrets/kek/status`). The page shows an inline note, rather than an empty list, when a panel is denied or cannot be read; a revocation list past its next-update time shows as expired rather than current. The Settings page and its Internal CA panel are unchanged; the revoke and import-chain buttons on both pages now show a toast when the server refuses them after the same-site check (a cross-origin refusal still shows nothing).
- **CRL figures on the certificate inventory.** `GET /api/v1/ca/issued` (`meta`) and the MCP `list_issued_certs` tool now carry `crl_version`, `crl_published_at`, `crl_next_update`, `crl_revoked_count` and `crl_stale`. Each is `null` when it could not be determined (a CRL published before counts were recorded has a `null` count, never `0`), so a client can judge revocation freshness without downloading the DER list.
