- **Dashboard readout, Needs attention and Estate state.** `/fragments/dashboard/summary` fills a
  four-cell readout (devices online, policy checks passing, failed executions in the last 24 hours,
  approvals waiting), a Needs attention list with links to the page that owns each item, and an
  Estate state panel (seen in 30 days, online, offline, not seen for 2 days or more). Each figure
  keeps its own permission, accounts limited to a group of devices see a "limited" note instead of
  fleet totals, and a figure that cannot be read says so rather than showing 0.
