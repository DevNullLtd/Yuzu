- **Breaking - `PUT /api/config/{key}` applies the same caller rule as the Settings routes for six administrator-only keys.** For `plugin_signing_required`, `dex_alert_routing`, `dex_blast_min_devices`, `dex_blast_window_seconds`, `dex_blast_cooldown_seconds` and `dex_cohort_export_key`, a session with an MCP-tier token (even one minted by an administrator), a service-scoped token, or a non-administrator effective role (for example a custom role that holds `Infrastructure:Write` under RBAC) now gets `403` with a `remediation` naming the Settings page; a temporary elevation still counts as administrator, as on the Settings routes. The refusal comes before the runtime-config store is opened and before the body is parsed, stores nothing, and is audited as `config.update` with result `denied` (`token_session_admin_setting_blocked` or `admin_role_required_setting_blocked`) and no preceding `attempted` row; the Settings routes audit the equivalent denial as `auth.admin_required`. The `oidc_*` keys keep their rule and now also refuse a service-scoped token. Every other key, including the retention keys, `log_level` and `heartbeat_timeout`, is unchanged and stays writable under `Infrastructure:Write`. Before this change these six keys could be written with `Infrastructure:Write` alone, so review their stored values (`GET /api/config`, then the `config.update` audit rows). Replaying the whole `GET /api/config` `overrides` map with a non-administrator token stops at the first administrator-only key. See [Upgrading](docs/user-manual/upgrading.md).
