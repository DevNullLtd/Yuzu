- **Server: application score policy core.** A pure, validated score policy that makes a customer's risk appetite data: the three lower tier edges and the four tier display names, the four deduction weights, and bounded per-application overrides keyed by lowercased image name that inherit anything they do not set. A resolver returns the effective policy for an application, and the stability score's band edges are now a parameter, so the same score and rank-flip helpers run under a customer's policy. Policies are refused whole with a typed reason (out-of-range or non-descending edges, a bad name, an invalid or oversized weight sum, a duplicate or empty application key, too many overrides). The shipped defaults reproduce the existing score exactly. Not yet surfaced: no store, route or page reads it, so there is no operator-visible change.
