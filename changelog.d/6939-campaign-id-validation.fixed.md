- **Access review campaign ids are validated centrally.** `GET /api/v1/access-reviews/{id}`, `POST .../{id}/attestations` and `POST .../{id}/close`, and the MCP tools `get_access_review`, `record_attestation` and `close_access_review`, now refuse an id that is not 1 to 64 characters of `A-Z`, `a-z`, `0-9`, `_` or `-` with a `400` (invalid-params on MCP) and write no audit row. A `%00` in the path previously reached the database bind as a real NUL byte and truncated the id; the store now refuses such ids, an embedded NUL in a campaign's `title` or creator, a decision's `principal_type`, `principal_id`, `role_name`, `justification` or reviewer, or a closer, and a `justification` over 4096 bytes, with a `bad_request:` error mapped to `400`.
