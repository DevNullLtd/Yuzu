- Approval tickets can now bind the exact reviewed effect and be redeemed only on the surface that minted them (#6062, four-eyes MVP PR2). `ApprovalManager::consume_ticket` takes the caller's declared origin (a caller that declares none, or a ticket with none, always refuses), has an overload that joins the caller's own transaction so a rollback undoes the consume, and `submit_bound` freezes a request, its SHA-256 digest and a list of reviewers barred from approving it (engine principals cannot submit one). The `guardian.` definition-id prefix is now reserved beside `mcp.`, the Approvals page shows a bound request's definition, targets and parameters instead of a bare digest, and every precondition denial is counted in `yuzu_approval_precondition_denied_total{origin}` and audited as `approval.precondition_denied`. The MCP approval-ticket recall behaves as before, except an MCP precondition denial now also leaves an `approval.precondition_denied` row.
