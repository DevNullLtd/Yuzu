- **Installed software syncs in a nightly window, and an overdue sync is spread rather than sent at once.** The regular daily sync runs at a stable per-agent moment between 01:00 and 05:00 on the endpoint's own clock and time zone (a built-in default; there is no setting yet), so a fleet in one time zone reports within four hours of the day. A sync more than 15 minutes overdue because the agent was off or the machine slept, a send that keeps failing, and a stored schedule more than two days ahead are spread over the next 30 minutes by a stable per-agent offset; a failing send retries every 30 seconds for up to 15 minutes, then pauses for that offset before the next burst.
