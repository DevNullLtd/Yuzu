- **`GET /api/v1/openapi.json` supports conditional requests.** Each form of the document (the default form and `?guides=true`) now carries its own strong `ETag` and `Cache-Control: no-cache`, and a request whose `If-None-Match` matches answers `304` with no body, after the `Infrastructure:Read` check. This spares the server re-sending and re-compressing the large document on every `/swagger` load. The comparison is exact: a weak validator, a list of validators or `*` is answered with the full document and a `200`.
