- **The server applies delta sections of the sectioned installed-software report, keeps a change history, and stores components.** A `delta` section is applied as row-hash removals and additions against the software the server holds, then checked against the section hash the device reports holding once it has applied the section (a new seventh field, `expect`, on the section record); a section that does not check out is undone on its own and asked for again in full, while the other sections of the report apply. Every stored installed-software section writes an install, removal or upgrade entry for what it changed (a component section writes none); a version is the epoch, version and release together, so a release-only or epoch-only update (a distribution backport, a Flatpak commit) is an upgrade, recorded as `[epoch:]version[-release]`. A re-report of the same software at the same epoch, version and release writes none, the first full section for a collector writes none, and a collector whose stored rows do not match its stored section hash (an out-of-band change to the table) starts over with no entries. Removed software is deleted outright, and entries older than 400 days are pruned under the retention clock guard. The `component_inventory` section is stored per device, and both new tables are removed with the device. New metric `yuzu_software_changes_total{kind}`. A delta or component section that does not verify, would leave a facet over the per-call record cap, or repeats a component kind and path is counted under the section outcome `mismatch`, and the warning alert `YuzuInventorySectionDeltaMismatch` fires when a facet has had at least 5 of them in a day and they are more than 5% of its sections; a repeated component kind and path (two components of one kind with an empty path, for example) fails on every resend and does not repair itself. No shipped agent sends the sectioned report, and no dashboard, API or MCP read shows the history or the components.
