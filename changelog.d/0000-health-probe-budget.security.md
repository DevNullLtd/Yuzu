- **`/health` and `/api/health` apply a per-source budget to requests that carry a credential.** A request with a `Cookie`, `Authorization` or `X-Yuzu-Token` header is counted against a per-source-address budget equal to `--rate-limit` (default 100 requests per second; values below 1 are treated as 1), shared by the two paths and separate from the REST budget, and is refused with `429`, `Retry-After: 1` and the standard error envelope once it is used up. The charge is made on the presence of the header, whether or not the credential is valid. Requests with no credential header resolve no session, are not counted, and are never refused by this limit; `/livez` and `/readyz` are unchanged. Every pre-routing rate-limit refusal now carries the standard error envelope (`correlation_id` and `retry_after_ms`): the general `--rate-limit` bucket, the login bucket (`--login-rate-limit`) and this health budget; every field those refusals had before is unchanged. Per-source budget entries are kept until the server restarts; purging them is planned follow-up work. Use `/livez` or `/readyz` for load balancer and orchestrator checks. See [Upgrading](docs/user-manual/upgrading.md).
