- **`/health` and `/api/health` apply a per-source budget to requests that carry a credential.** A request with a `Cookie`, `Authorization` or `X-Yuzu-Token` header is counted against a per-source-address budget equal to `--rate-limit` (default 100 requests per second; values below 1 are treated as 1), shared by the two paths and separate from the REST budget, and is refused with `429`, `Retry-After: 1` and the standard error envelope once it is used up. Requests with no credential header resolve no session, are not counted, and are never refused by this limit; `/livez` and `/readyz` are unchanged. Use `/livez` or `/readyz` for load balancer and orchestrator checks. See [Upgrading](docs/user-manual/upgrading.md).
