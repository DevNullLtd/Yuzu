- **All six colour schemes now apply to every chart and to the fleet 3D view.** Chart series 6-12 were dark-tuned pastels on the three light schemes (about 1.3-2:1 on white); they are now darkened to 4.5:1 or better, and a new contrast gate holds every light-scheme text, chart and on-accent pair to 4.5:1. The Fleet view's OS and process-category colours and its label plates are read from the active scheme's tokens instead of fixed slate hex values, and the scheme-name whitelist used by the page boot scripts and the Settings picker is now a single constant.
