- **Changing an account's role needs your own administrator role.** `POST /api/settings/users/{username}/role` now refuses, with `403` and audit `user.role_change / denied / elevated_session_role_change_blocked`, a session that is administrator only through a temporary elevation. An account role is a standing grant; an elevation stays temporary. Names made only of dots (`.`, `..`) are no longer accepted when creating an account, since they cannot be addressed in a request path.
