- **Every fleet-gated and list-gated read now answers a retryable `503`, not a `403`, when its authorization read fails.** The shared list-read check reports a failed read (the global-grant, permission-group or management-group read, or an engine principal's grant read) separately from "no grant"; the fleet-read gate and `require_list_read` answer `503` with `retry_after_ms: 5000` (no `permission` named) and write an `auth.fleet_read_required` or `auth.permission_required` row with `result=failure`. Both answers still deny. A `require_list_read` route with a management-group store that is not wired or not open is `503` too, as the fleet-read gate already was. The upload-grants list resolvers still map a failed read to deny. A client that treats `403` as terminal should retry on `503` after the interval.
