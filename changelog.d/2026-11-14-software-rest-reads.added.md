- **Five REST reads on the software inventory: hosts, one host, the change feed, a summary and collection health (REST only for now).** `GET /api/v1/inventory/software/hosts` lists the devices that report software with their last report time and per-section state. `GET /api/v1/inventory/software/hosts/{agent_id}` returns one device's sections and its installs. `GET /api/v1/inventory/software/changes` is a cursor-paged feed of installs, removals and upgrades in the order they were observed; rows younger than 60 seconds are withheld and `stable_before` says where the feed is complete. `GET /api/v1/inventory/software/summary` gives headline counts. `GET /api/v1/inventory/software/plugin-health` shows, per collection section, how many devices are current, timing out, constrained, disabled or not collected, with duration and size percentiles and the slowest devices. All five need `Inventory:Read` and are confined to the caller's management-group scope. New gauges `yuzu_software_facet_devices` and `yuzu_software_facet_duration_ms`, and two alert rules on sustained timeout share and failure share.
