- **A partially installed product pack no longer answers a retry as a clean success.** `POST /api/product-packs` records its `Idempotency-Key` only when every item installed. A `201` that carried `errors[]` leaves the key unrecorded, so the key is never answered as a clean replay of the half-installed pack. Do not retry a `201` that carries `errors[]`: delete the partial pack and reinstall. A complete install still replays.
