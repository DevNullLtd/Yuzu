- **Breaking — a user-store fault in the provenance or role check before a SCIM deactivate, delete, reactivate or update, and in the role re-check after a `POST` revive's reactivation, is now a retryable `503`, not a `404` (#6836).** The IdP used to be told `404` (audit `scim.user.provenance_denied` / `denied`) for a deprovision that never ran, or, after a `PUT`/`PATCH` that had already changed `active`, one that had already completed. Yuzu now answers `503` with `Retry-After: 2` and audits the request's own action (`scim.user.deactivated`, `deleted`, `reactivated`, `updated`, or `scim.user.provisioned` with `store_unavailable: revive role check` for a `POST` revive, after undoing the reactivation) as `failure` with detail `store_unavailable: provenance check` or `role check`. SIEM rules keyed on `scim.user.provenance_denied` no longer see these outages and rules on `failure` now match them. The denial counter and the `scim.user.deprovision_role_refused_with_link` escalation no longer fire on a fault; a new counter `yuzu_scim_guard_store_unavailable_total{guard="provenance"|"role"}` and a new `YuzuScimGuardStoreUnavailable` alert cover it. A `PUT`, or a `PATCH` that sets `externalId` with an `active` change, applies the `active` change first, so its `503` can follow an already-applied change (the retry completes). A `POST` revive whose undo also fails during a real outage answers `500` with `scim.user.compensation_failed` and may leave the account active: check it. **Still not covered** (a full outage answering `401` at bearer validation was closed by #6835, which now answers `503`): the `POST` create reads (#6837), the tombstone-role `500` in the revive pre-check and the `500` when the reactivation's own re-read fails (same defect class as #6837, which does not list them), no reconciliation sweep (#6838), and the #5214 state (`404` plus the D1 escalation on every retry). Configure the connector to retry `5xx`; details in `docs/user-manual/upgrading.md` and `docs/user-manual/scim-provisioning.md`.
