- **TAR `registry` source names the process that wrote a change.** `$Registry_Live` rows now carry `actor_pid` and `actor_image`: a write-only Kernel-Registry ETW session (value writes, value deletions and key deletions only) reports which process wrote a value, and each change the key watch finds is matched to the writes of the same value name, type and size inside the window in which it must have happened. `detail` says how the actor was reached: `attr:corr` when exactly one process wrote a matching value, and, with no process named, `attr:ambiguous:<n>`, `attr:none`, `attr:degraded` (write events were lost, with a `gap:loss:<n>` row), `attr:off` or `attr:lost-restart`. The actor is a lead to verify against `$Process_Live`, not proof. The ETW session needs an elevated agent; without one the key watch runs alone and rows say `attr:off`. A row now lands one slow tick after the one that sees the change, once its writer is settled, and the change waits in the source's cursor row (old and new value readable through `tar.sql`) until then. `tar.status` gains `registry_etw_events`, `registry_etw_dropped`, `registry_etw_lost`, `registry_attr_attributed`, `registry_attr_ambiguous` and `registry_attr_none`, and `registry_capture_method` reads `notify+etw` while the session runs.
