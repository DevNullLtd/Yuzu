- **Access-review attestations require the reviewer to differ from the subject of the grant, and every decision is kept.** `POST /api/v1/access-reviews/{id}/attestations` and the MCP tool `record_attestation` now refuse a decision (`403`; permission-denied on MCP, with the message `the reviewer must differ from the subject of this grant; ask another reviewer to record this decision`) when the grant is a `user` grant for the reviewer's own account, an engine principal the reviewer owns, or a group the reviewer belongs to; the engine and group checks are read just before the write, fail closed (`503`) when they cannot be answered, and a refusal writes an `access_review.attested` or `access_review.flagged` row with `result=denied` and counts in `yuzu_access_review_attestation_refused_total{surface,reason}`. A later decision no longer replaces an earlier one: each is appended to a new `access_review_attestation_history` table (access-review store schema v3), and `GET /api/v1/access-reviews/{id}` and `get_access_review` return `decision_seq` and `history`. The attestation audit rows now carry the campaign id alone as `target_id` and the grant in `detail`. A database that holds a decision recorded by the subject of the grant makes the server refuse to start, so deploy into a fresh database.
