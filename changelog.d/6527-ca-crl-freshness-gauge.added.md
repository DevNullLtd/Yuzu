- **The server now exports the internal CA's CRL expiry and publish time, and alerts before the CRL runs out (#6527).** `/metrics` carries `yuzu_server_ca_crl_next_update_timestamp_seconds` and `yuzu_server_ca_crl_published_timestamp_seconds` (absent until a CRL has been published; remaining time is the first minus `time()`). New alerts `YuzuCaCrlNearExpiry` (warning, under 12 hours left for 10 minutes) and `YuzuCaCrlExpired` (critical, past `nextUpdate`) fire when the leader's freshness pass has stopped republishing, which previously showed nowhere until a relying party rejected the CRL. No flags, no migration. Reload your Prometheus rule file to pick the alerts up and review the new critical rule before routing it; an install with its own certificates has no internal CA and exports nothing.
