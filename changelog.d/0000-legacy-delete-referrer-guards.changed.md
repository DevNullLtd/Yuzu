- **Breaking: the released `DELETE /api/instructions/{id}` and `DELETE /api/product-packs/{id}` now refuse a definition that is still referenced.** A definition that a schedule, a live workflow or a policy fragment still names was previously deleted out from under them. `DELETE /api/instructions/{id}` now answers `409` listing the referrers (nothing deleted; one `instruction.delete` row, `denied` / `referenced`), and `503` when the reference check cannot be answered or the definition cannot be read first. `DELETE /api/product-packs/{id}` runs the same check for every InstructionDefinition item the pack carries before it deletes any item (the pack's own workflows are not counted, nor are its own policy fragments unless a policy still uses one, which survives the uninstall, is counted and has the policy named in the `409`) and answers `409` or `503` the same way, so a pack is never half-uninstalled. The check is advisory, not transactional. Installed product packs that contain a deleted definition do not block it: the `instruction.delete` success row (legacy, v1 and MCP) ends with `packs=<id>,...` (at most 5, then `,+N`).
