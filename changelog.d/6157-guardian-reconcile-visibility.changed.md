- **A Guardian heartbeat reconcile that throws is now counted, and a rejected Guardian push now names every store it depends on.** The new label-free counter `yuzu_server_guardian_reconcile_exceptions_total` (pre-seeded to 0) counts each time the reconcile hook throws out of the reconcile flow into the heartbeat handler; the handler still catches the exception, logs a warning and carries on with the rest of that heartbeat, and `YuzuGuardianReconcileDegraded` now also fires when the counter keeps rising. The `503` of `POST /api/v1/guaranteed-state/push` and the retryable internal error of the MCP tool `push_guardian_rules` that report a degraded store now read "a store the push depends on is degraded (guaranteed-state, Baseline, tag, custom-property or management-group)" instead of the bare "guaranteed-state store degraded"; status codes, `retry_after_ms` and the audit prefix are unchanged. The start-up warning for a clamped `--guardian-reconcile-budget-ms-per-s`, `--guardian-reconcile-burst-ms` or `--guardian-reconcile-max-per-s` keeps its wording.
