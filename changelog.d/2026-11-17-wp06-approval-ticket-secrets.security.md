- **MCP approval tickets no longer store raw secret arguments.** A supervised-tier call to an approval-gated tool bound its full arguments into `approvals.scope_expression` (readable by every `Approval:Read` holder and kept in Postgres and its backups). `secret` (`confirm_engine_rotation`, `confirm_api_token_rotation`) and `grant_secret`, `grant_id` and `client_secret` (including inside `params` and bundle `steps`) are now replaced by a `<redacted:sha256:...>` marker before the ticket is minted; a recall with a different secret still mismatches the ticket. A one-off migration (approval store v5) blanks the subject of every existing MCP ticket that names those keys and expires the pending ones. **Rotate any engine credential, API-token successor secret or Entra `client_secret` that was ever sent through a supervised MCP call, and treat database backups as still holding the old values.**
