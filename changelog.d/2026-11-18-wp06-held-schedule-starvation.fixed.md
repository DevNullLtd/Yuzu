- **Schedules held on an unavailable auth store no longer starve the schedules behind them.** A schedule whose arming check was unavailable is skipped without being asked for 90 seconds, and no longer counts against the three-asks-per-tick cap once held, so a schedule that needs no store read (a Read-class one) runs during the outage, and the scheduler rotates its starting point through the due list so lapsed holds at the head cannot keep pre-empting the tail. A failed due-schedule read leaves the holds alone, and a replica that is no longer the leader zeroes `yuzu_schedule_due_held`. New `yuzu_schedule_arming_deferred_total` counter, `yuzu_schedule_due_held` gauge and `YuzuScheduleDueHeld` alert. The two arming-unavailable alerts now use a 5-minute window with a 15-minute hold, so a short fault that has already cleared no longer pages.
