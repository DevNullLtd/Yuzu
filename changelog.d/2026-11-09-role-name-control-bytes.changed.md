- **Breaking - role names containing a control byte are refused by the role removal and engine-role tools.** MCP `unassign_rbac_role`, and the engine-role assign and unassign (REST `POST`/`DELETE /api/v1/engine-principals/{id}/roles` and the MCP `assign_engine_role`/`unassign_engine_role` twins), refuse a role name containing a NUL, other control byte (0x01-0x1F) or DEL: REST answers `400`, MCP answers invalid params (-32602), and the audit row is `result=denied` with the detail `invalid_role_name`. Any other role name is accepted unchanged, including one granted out of band. A deployment that holds a role created out of band with such a byte in its name must delete or rename it in SQL; see the upgrade note in `docs/user-manual/upgrading.md` for the query that finds them.
