- **The Linux DEX journald source now logs how many entries failed the trusted-sender check and when its cursor re-baselines.** The first poll that drops entries, then at most one poll an hour, logs `dropped journal entries failing the trusted-sender check` with a running total; user-manager entries are filtered by the journalctl match before the check and are not counted, and a journalctl failure that re-baselines the cursor logs a gap line (`gap #N since start`) noting that entries between the previous cursor and the journal end were not observed. These are log lines, not metrics. They make a wrong coredump identity, or a rotation/vacuum gap, visible.
