- **An Echo cursor source whose ticks keep failing no longer re-reads everything on every tick, and shows that it is stuck.** The power, removable and registry sources are retried one tick, then 2, 4 and 8 ticks later and every 12 ticks after that (collect output `retry_backoff`; `status` shows `<source>_next_retry_at`), and the registry source records the stretch it could not commit as a `gap:commit_stalled` row on its next successful commit.
