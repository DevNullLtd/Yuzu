- **RBAC enforcement, your elevation and an own-permission check on the Users and roles page.** An **Enforce roles** switch shows the server's setting to every signed-in user and lets an administrator turn it on or off with a confirmation and an MFA step-up; the switch is always redrawn from the server, shows its refusals with their remediation, and reads **Unconfirmed** when the setting cannot be confirmed. **Your elevation** raises or ends your own temporary admin session with a required reason, and **Check your own permission** asks whether your roles grant an area and operation.
