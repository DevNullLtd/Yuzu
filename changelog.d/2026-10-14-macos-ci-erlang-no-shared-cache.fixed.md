- **macOS CI no longer races its two runner agents on the Erlang install.** The macOS job used `erlef/setup-beam`, which rmRF's and re-copies one `/opt/ci/tool_cache/otp/...` directory shared by both BigMags agents on every run, so a debug + release pair starting together intermittently failed (`EEXIST` on the `epmd` symlink, `ENOENT` mid-copy, `Unable to locate executable file: erl`; 36 of the 79 macOS job failures between 2026-10-03 and 10-13). The job now downloads the pinned prebuilt OTP 28.5.0.7 and rebar3 3.24.0 into the per-job `RUNNER_TEMP`, verifies both against pinned SHA-256s, and puts them on `PATH`. The Linux leg is unchanged.
