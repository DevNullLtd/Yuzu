- local_security_policy plugin: local password, lockout and audit policy posture (`password_policy`, `lockout_policy`, `audit_policy`), read-only. Linux reads `/etc/login.defs`, `/etc/security/pwquality.conf`/`faillock.conf`, `/etc/pam.d/*` and `/etc/audit/audit.rules`; macOS runs `pwpolicy -getaccountpolicies` and reads `/etc/security/audit_control`. A refused read reports `PERMISSION_DENIED`/`CONSTRAINED` with a named token, never absent. Windows and the `sudoers` action are planned and follow as their own PR — until then Windows reports one planned-state row rather than an empty success.
