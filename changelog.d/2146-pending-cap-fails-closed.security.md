- **The per-principal pending-approval cap now refuses when the approval store cannot be read, instead of admitting the caller.** The cap that bounds one principal's share of the shared approval queue (25 pending approvals, applied to `POST /api/v1/instructions/{id}/execute`, the MCP `execute_instruction_definition` tool and the MCP approval-ticket mint of every approval-gated tool) read the principal's pending count with a call that returned `0` on a pool timeout or a failed query, so a struggling approval store admitted every principal at once. An unreadable count now refuses the call with `503` `approval_unavailable` on REST and `-32603` `approval_unavailable` on MCP, both with `retry_after_ms` 5000, creates no approval or ticket, and is counted on `yuzu_server_approval_submit_refused_total{reason="approval_unavailable"}`. The count still spans every approval origin, so the cap remains a bound on the principal's total share of the queue.
