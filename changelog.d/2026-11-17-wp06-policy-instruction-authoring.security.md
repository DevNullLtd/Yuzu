- **A policy fragment's instructions are authorized when it is stored (interim for #6065).** `POST /api/policy-fragments` and a PolicyFragment item of `POST /api/product-packs` require the principal to be authorized, through the same dispatch dry run as `execute_instruction`, for the `plugin.action` of its check, postCheck and fix instructions (`403` otherwise, nothing stored; `503` when the instruction store is unreadable or the authorization is unwired). The authorized pairs are stored with the fragment (policy store schema v6) and the store refuses a fragment they do not cover; an instruction later edited to a different `plugin.action` is refused at dispatch (the check writes an `error` verdict, `409` on evaluate and remediate, `yuzu_server_policy_instruction_pair_mismatch_total`). Manual remediate re-checks the fix pair and limits targets to the operator's visible devices. The identity a policy runs as (the system caller) is unchanged, and a fragment stored by a principal who is later demoted keeps running.
