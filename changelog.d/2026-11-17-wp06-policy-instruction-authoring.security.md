- **Policy fragments authorize their instructions at authoring time, and manual remediate honours device visibility (interim for #6065).** Creating a fragment (`POST /api/policy-fragments`) now requires the author to be authorized, through the same dispatch dry run as `execute_instruction`, for the `plugin.action` of its check, postCheck and fix instructions (`403` otherwise, nothing stored; `503` if the instruction store is unreadable). `POST /api/policies/{id}/remediate` re-checks the fix pair for the operator and limits the targets to the devices the operator may execute on. The identity a policy runs as at fire time (the system caller) is unchanged.
