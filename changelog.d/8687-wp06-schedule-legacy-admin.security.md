- **A schedule's creator must hold the legacy administrator role at fire time for a non-Read action when RBAC is off.** A Read-class action still fires; an engine principal never does. If the auth store cannot answer (including a failed permission read under enforced RBAC), the occurrence is held and retried (`yuzu_schedule_arming_unavailable_total`, `yuzu_server_command_outbox_deliver_arming_unavailable_total`), not recorded as denied. Administrators cannot delete or disable another user's schedule.
