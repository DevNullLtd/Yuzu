- **Agentic workers can now read fleet application usage over MCP.** The new `get_dex_apps_usage` tool is the twin of `GET /api/v1/dex/apps/usage`: for each application on the Digital Employee Experience Apps list it returns how many devices ran it, its run time and launches over a 30-day basis and its crashes and hangs per hour of use, split into fleet, platform, group or tag cells, and the body is the same as the route's. It needs both `GuaranteedState:Read` and `Forensics:Read`, is readable at every MCP tier and is refused to service-scoped tokens. Every admitted call writes a `dex.apps.usage.view` audit row before the read, and the tool does not read if that row cannot be stored. The REST route now maps a refused read to its answer through the same shared function as the tool, with unchanged responses.
