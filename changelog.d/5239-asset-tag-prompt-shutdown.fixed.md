- **The `asset_tags` plugin no longer holds up agent shutdown or plugin unload by up to 5 seconds.** Its background staleness-check thread slept in fixed 5-second slices and only looked at the shutdown flag between them, so every shutdown waited out the remainder of a slice. The thread now wakes the moment shutdown is requested. The staleness check itself, its interval, and the `status`/`sync` output are unchanged.
