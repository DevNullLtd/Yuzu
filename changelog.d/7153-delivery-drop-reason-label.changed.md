- **Behaviour change: the webhook and offload delivery drop counters now carry a `reason` label, and each store's delivery backlog is published.** `yuzu_server_webhook_delivery_dropped_total` and `yuzu_server_offload_delivery_dropped_total` split by `reason` (`queue_full` or `quiescing`), so a full queue can be told from a shutdown; both reasons are present at `0` from boot. A query that selected the old unlabelled series without `sum` now returns two series per server. See `docs/user-manual/upgrading.md`.
