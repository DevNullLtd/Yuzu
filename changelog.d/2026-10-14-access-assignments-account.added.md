- **Role assignments, Assign/Remove and Your account on the Users and roles page.** A **Role assignments** panel lists every fleet-wide grant (it needs `AccessReview:Read` and writes the same `rbac.assignments.list` audit row as the REST call). Administrators can **Assign role** and **Remove** a plain user grant; the server's refusals (last administrator, your own Administrator grant) are shown as they came, and the panel reloads after a server error because the change may have taken effect. The Users table gains an **RBAC roles** column and the Roles table an **Assigned** column, both derived from that panel and shown as "Not shown" unless the grant table was read in full. A **Your account** panel offers **Change password** (local accounts) and **Sign out everywhere**.
