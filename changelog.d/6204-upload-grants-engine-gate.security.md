- **Engine principals are default-deny in `GET /api/v1/upload-grants` and in the shared per-agent visibility resolvers.** An engine principal needs RBAC enabled and an explicit grant (`UploadGrant:Read` to list upload grants): with RBAC off or no grant it gets `403` (`503` when the RBAC store is unavailable), the same answer as the MCP `list_upload_grants` tool, and the shared resolvers no longer treat it as legacy-open when RBAC is off. Human sessions are unchanged.
