- **Breaking — an MFA enrolment or recovery-code refusal caused by the host's random-number generator is now audited as `csprng_unavailable` and counted in `yuzu_secure_random_failure_total`, no longer as a store outage (#6995).** The refusal is still fail-closed and the status code, `Retry-After` header and operator message are unchanged, but the audit `detail` of `mfa.enroll.required`, `mfa.enroll.failed`, `mfa.enroll.initiated` and `mfa.recovery_codes.generated` now starts `csprng_unavailable: `, and the sign-in `mfa.enroll.failed` event `reason` is `csprng_unavailable` instead of `store_unavailable`. The fault is counted in the new series `yuzu_secure_random_failure_total{reason="prng_failure",site="mfa_enroll"|"mfa_recovery"}` and is no longer counted in `yuzu_auth_secret_unavailable_total` or `yuzu_auth_read_degrade_total{reason="query_error"}`; a Settings recovery-code regeneration refused because the auth store failed now increments the new `yuzu_auth_secret_unavailable_total{route="mfa_recovery"}`. A random-number failure while drawing a recovery code's salt, which previously escaped as a bare `500` with no audit row, now takes the same audited, counted fail-closed path. All six `site` series of `yuzu_secure_random_failure_total` are now created at 0 at boot. SIEM or webhook rules keyed on the old reason or detail text, and Prometheus rules that relied on the old counters to see this fault, must be re-keyed; during a rolling upgrade both signal sets appear. See `docs/user-manual/upgrading.md`, `docs/user-manual/metrics.md` and `docs/ops-runbooks/auth-db-recovery.md`.
