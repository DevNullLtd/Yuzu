- **Gateway holds stream-status notifications instead of dropping them.** When the 10-notification in-flight limit was reached or the upstream circuit breaker was not closed, the gateway dropped `CONNECTED` and `DISCONNECTED` notifications, which could leave agents acknowledged by the server but not dispatchable until they reconnected. They are now held in a bounded pending store keyed by agent, session and stream home, replaced in place by newer events for the same key, and sent first in first out. While the breaker is half open exactly one held notification is sent as the probe; success closes the breaker and drains the rest, failure reopens it and requeues the probe's event. One in-flight slot stays free for an active registration replay. New gauges `yuzu_gw_upstream_notify_pending` and `yuzu_gw_upstream_notify_inflight`, counter `yuzu_gw_upstream_notify_coalesced_total`, and setting `notify_pending_max` (default 10000); `yuzu_gw_upstream_notify_dropped_total` now counts only `at_capacity`. Refs #4632
