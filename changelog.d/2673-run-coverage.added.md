- **Execution results now say who a run was meant for and what happened to each device.** `GET /api/v1/executions/{id}` and the MCP tool `get_execution_status` carry a `coverage` object: intended, contacted, responded, failed, timed out, offline, not sent, withheld, pending and late counts, and whether only connected agents were considered. Management-group-confined callers see counts over their visible devices only. A silent device is marked timed out after the definition's `gather_ttl_seconds` (at least 660 seconds without a signal), by a new server sweep that writes `execution.deadline_sweep` audit rows. New metrics: `yuzu_exec_tracker_deadline_sweep_total`, `yuzu_exec_tracker_conflicting_final_total`. Runs created before this change, or whose targets could not be recorded, read `unrecorded`.
