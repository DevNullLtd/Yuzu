## Decision lines

- **Partitioning.** `installed_software` stays a plain table. No measurement exists (the S-5 spike was skipped). Hash-partitioning by `agent_id` needs the non-transactional migration kind that `app_perf_daily_store.hpp` and the ladder row say is not built, would rewrite the table under `ACCESS EXCLUSIVE` past the v7 2-4M-row ceiling, and would turn the one measured query (the `installed_software_name_idx` seek) into one scan per partition. Reversible: revisit when a measurement exists.
- **Uniqueness.** The enforced constraint is `(agent_id, instance_key)` over a partial index, not an identity-keyed `UNIQUE`: one MSI product is listed by both `msi_packages` and `installed_apps` and the two are not de-duplicated, so an identity key would refuse a Windows host's first v10 ingest. `identity_key` is stored and derived, not unique.
- **Cutover posture.** The unique-index build is not online. It is accepted under the same hard-cutover posture the ladder records for v7; the measured build time at 1.8M rows is in the ladder row.
- **Placeholders.** Delta apply, the `-` deletion apply, and component storage are not in this change: a delta and a component section are answered with a per-section need-full and write no rows, so nothing is lost and no resource is held for them.
