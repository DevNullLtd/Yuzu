## Deadlines (disclosed residual)

The lookup and the write are synchronous audit-store calls on the heartbeat ingest thread with no deadline of their own. The change bounds how many may run at once (4, one PostgreSQL pool lease each) and records a throwing, degraded or failed call (the gate-wide 60 s window, or only that agent's own wait when the gate already remembers it as failing), but a slow-but-succeeding call opens no window and blocks its ingest thread for as long as it takes (finding g4-up1).
