## Decision lines

- **No reparse point followed at any component.** The only path-based open is `CreateFileW` of the drive root; everything below is one `NtCreateFile` relative to that handle with `OBJ_DONT_REPARSE`; an entry with `FILE_ATTRIBUTE_REPARSE_POINT` is typed `link` and never opened. Open finding at this review: `DRIVE_FIXED` is checked at root planning only, not at open time (finding `G3-SG-1`); the fix adds a `GetDriveTypeW` call before each open.
- **Bounds.** One run: at most 400,000 directory entries across all roots, 45 s wall (checked between directories and files, no per-call deadline: a disclosed residual shared with `runtimes`), 5,000 native rows, 16 MiB per read.
- **Names are lossless.** WTF-8 round-trips exactly (fuzzed under ASan/UBSan at this review); the row writer maps non-UTF-8 to `?`.
