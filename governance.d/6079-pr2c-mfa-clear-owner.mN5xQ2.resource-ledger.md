## Statement

Production C++ adds no fd, HANDLE, SOCKET, `FILE*`, sqlite3 object, OpenSSL or BCrypt object, allocated C string, mapped library, temp path, subprocess or thread, and no manual cleanup. It adds one pool lease and one transaction per owner commit (both RAII through `PgPool::with_txn_for`), one owner type that borrows the pool and the audit store, one raw borrowed pointer in `AuthManager`, two `std::function` callback seams, and in-memory epoch increments under the existing `mu_`. Every PG result is a `pg::PgResult`.
