## Exception behaviour

- `http_response_policy`: a `std::bad_alloc` from the buffer or the assign leaves `res.body` unmodified and propagates to httplib's handler exception boundary (500); an encoder failure sends the original body, identity-encoded.
- The Apps read: a statement cancelled by the statement timeout (SQLSTATE 57014) is `kStatementTimeout`, any other failed statement `kQueryError`; both are values, not exceptions.
- `DexApi::apps`: the observer call can no longer discard a good read; a throw leaves `observer_state` unset and logs one throttled line (R9).
- The Apps route: any exception from the `DexApi` or the renderer renders a visible `internal_error` panel with HTTP 200 and one sampled error line (R10); previously it reached httplib as an empty 500 that htmx dropped. REST and MCP still have no containment around `apps()` (a throw there is a 500 or a tool error, as before; chaos UP-4 noted it).
